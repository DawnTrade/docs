Your **balance** is the USDC you buy coins, place orders and withdraw with. Your **wallet** is the addresses themselves: anything sitting there can't be used until you move it across.

## What ends up in the wallet

Anything that arrives outside a [deposit route](/funds/deposit):

- **SOL** sent to your Solana address. Dawn messages you when it arrives.
- **Coins sent to you**, by someone else or by you from another wallet.
- **A refunded deposit** can land here.
- **Leftover native coin**: small amounts of a chain's own coin from earlier activity.

## Move it across

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the coin">
    In `/wallets`, anything in a wallet is listed under its chain. Or tap the message Dawn sent when it arrived.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm">
    Check roughly how much USDC lands on your balance, and confirm. If it fails, it stays in your wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Moving is a trade

Anything that isn't USDC is swapped, so the market price decides what you get, thin liquidity costs you more, and the amount shown is an estimate until it lands.

<Tip>
  To keep a coin rather than convert it, leave it in the wallet or [send it out](/funds/withdraw#send-a-coin-instead-of-usdc) as it is.
</Tip>
