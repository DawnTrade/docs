Send `/withdraw`, or `/send` for the menu. You can send **USDC**, a chain's own coin, or **any token you hold**.

## Withdraw to an address

<Steps>
  <Step title="Normal or private">
    Tap **📤 Normal withdrawal** to send straight to the address. For **🕶 Private withdrawal**, see [Private withdrawals](#private-withdrawals).
  </Step>

  <Step title="The chain it arrives on">
    The recipient's network, not yours: any [chain Dawn trades on](/chains) — Solana, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Base, Arc, Ink or HyperEVM — or Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism or Polygon.
  </Step>

  <Step title="What should arrive">
    On Solana, **⭐ USDC** goes straight from your balance. Any other token is swapped on the way out.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The recipient">
    Paste the address, or pick one of your own [wallets or profiles](#send-to-your-own-wallets). Add several and the amount is **split evenly** between them.
  </Step>

  <Step title="The amount">
    Type it, or `max` for all of it. Anything other than USDC on Solana needs at least **\$1 per recipient**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Approve">
    Tap **✅ Approve in Wallet** and approve the send in the wallet app. Nothing leaves until you do.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Approve it in the wallet app

Every send and withdrawal is approved by you in the wallet app, and one you don't approve within **5 minutes** expires. With [2FA](/wallets/two-factor) on, Privy asks for it first.

<Warning>
  Check the address and the chain yourself. A sent transaction cannot be recalled, and an address that is right on one network can be wrong on another. Never paste a token's contract address as the recipient.
</Warning>

<Info>
  A send marked **⏳ Sent** is still confirming. Wait for it rather than starting a second withdrawal. If one didn't go through, see [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
</Info>

## What it costs

**Dawn charges no fee to withdraw or send.** You pay the gas and, if the recipient has never held the token, the cost of creating their token account.

| Route | What you pay |
| --- | --- |
| **From Solana** | The gas, plus the token account if needed. Paid in USDC from your balance, even when you send SOL or another token. |
| **From an EVM chain** | The gas, paid in that chain's own coin from your wallet on that chain. Keep a little of it there. |
| **To another chain** | The gas, in USDC. The confirmation shows the amount that will arrive. |
| **Private** | The exchanges' fees and the network cost, taken out of what you send. The quote shows the fees and what arrives. |

The gas comes on top of the amount you type, unless you send `max`.

## Send to your own wallets

The recipient picker lists your own profiles and wallets. Use it to fund a new wallet, move money between profiles, or gather funds from several wallets into one. Pick several to split an amount across them.

## Send a coin instead of USDC

To move a coin without selling it, pick it instead of USDC. It stays on its own chain and arrives as the same coin.

<Info>
  Coins bought on HyperEVM through Dawn can't be sent out as they are: sell them, and the USDC lands on your balance. Some other coins' contracts restrict transfers, so not every balance you can see can be moved.
</Info>

## Private withdrawals

A private withdrawal is routed through Husher and paid out by an exchange, so it can't be traced back to your wallet. It comes out of your USDC balance, and arrives as the token you pick on the chain you pick.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Turn Private on">
    Tap **🕶 Private withdrawal** in `/withdraw`, or tap **🕶 Private: Off** on the send screen to turn it on. Then pick the chain and what should arrive.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the addresses">
    Paste up to **100**, one a line, or pick your own wallets or profiles.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter an amount">
    The screen shows the minimum, fees included. With several addresses, it applies to each one, plus the network cost once.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Shape the send">
    Optional. Change how the legs are [split and timed, and which exchange pays](#split-timing-and-exchange).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm and approve">
    Tap **🕶 Send privately** and confirm, then tap **🔐 Approve in app** and approve the deposit. Nothing is sent until you approve, and the send lapses **30 minutes** after you confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Once it is deposited, a private send cannot be recalled. Check every address before you approve.
</Warning>

### Split, timing and exchange

| Setting | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **🔀 Split** | Sends each address's amount in several legs, each a different size |
| **⏲ Timing** | Spreads the legs over a window of up to 24 hours, each at a random time |
| **🔁 Exchange** | Which exchange pays the legs out. **Auto** picks one for each leg, or pick one to pay them all |
| **🎚 Spread** | How far each leg's size can stray from an even share. **20%** unless you change it |
| **🎲 Reroll** | New random sizes and times for the legs |

To change one leg's exchange, its delay after the deposit (up to 24 hours) or its amount, tap **✏️ Edit legs**, or **Edit** beside the leg. Your edits stay when you reroll.

A private send carries up to **100 legs** across all its addresses. Each exchange has a minimum per leg, and every leg must clear it before you can send.

### Following a private send

Tap **⏩ Send now** on the send's message to pay out the legs still waiting on their delay.

A send that was never deposited, or a leg that wasn't sent, leaves its USDC in your wallet. For a leg marked ⚠️ failed or ↩️ refunded, contact [support](/help/support) with the reference on the message.
