Send `/deposit`, or tap **Deposit** on the home screen.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the receiving wallet">
    Only asked when you have more than one. The deposit lands on this wallet's balance.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the chain you're sending from">
    See [which chains you can send from](#which-chains-you-can-send-from).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick the token">
    Only the tokens listed for that chain are credited.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Send to the address">
    Send from your own wallet or exchange, using **the same token on the same network**. The panel shows the route's **minimum**: check it before you send.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Dawn messages you **Deposit received** once it's on your balance.

<Warning>
  Send only the token and the chain the panel names. Anything else cannot be recovered. Each chain has its own address, so always copy it fresh from the panel rather than reusing one from another chain.
</Warning>

## Which chains you can send from

The [chains Dawn trades on](/chains) — Solana, BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Base, Arc, Ink and HyperEVM — plus Ethereum, Arbitrum, Optimism and Polygon. A route can be unavailable for a while, so the deposit panel has the live list.

## The free route

**USDC on Solana** goes straight onto your balance: no swap, no bridge, no fee, credited as soon as it confirms. From an exchange, it's the cheapest way in.

Everything else is swapped and bridged on the way in. That cost comes out of the amount that lands, and the panel estimates it before you send.

## Deposits that land in your wallet instead

**SOL** sent to your Solana address, and **any coin** transferred to you outside a deposit route, land in your wallet rather than on your balance. They aren't spendable USDC until you [move them to your balance](/funds/move-to-balance).

## Buying with a card

Tap **💳 Buy with card** on the deposit screen, then **Open MoonPay**. MoonPay is set to buy **USDC on Solana** into the wallet you're depositing into. It shows its fees before you pay, and the USDC lands on your balance a few minutes after.

<Tip>
  Buying elsewhere? Any exchange works: withdraw to your Dawn deposit address, choosing **USDC** and the **Solana** network.
</Tip>

## If the balance has not changed

- Check the address, the chain, and which wallet you deposited into.
- **Ready** on the home screen only counts wallets in the profile you're in. If the wallet is in another profile, switch with **Switch profile** on the home screen.
- A deposit sent long after you opened the panel can land without a message, so check **Ready** too.
- A coin in your portfolio with your balance unchanged means you received a coin, not USDC — see [above](#deposits-that-land-in-your-wallet-instead).

If a route fails or is refunded, Dawn messages you with the reason. Still stuck? See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) or [@DawnContactBot](https://t.me/DawnContactBot).
