Find it in `/settings` → **Group Links**. It's set per profile, and **off** by default.

<Info>
  This is about links you tap. To post your own, see [Share links](/social/share-links). Pasting an address yourself is [Auto Buy](/settings/overview#auto-buy), a separate setting.
</Info>

## What a tap does

With group links on, you also choose a **buy amount**: an amount of your own, or **Card Amount** (the default), the size on the card.

|  | Your own amount | **Card Amount** | Group links off |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A buy link** | Buys your amount, from this profile's wallet | Buys the card's amount, up to \$10,000 | Opens the coin |
| **A sell link** | Sells the share the link names of what you hold | Opens the sell panel | Opens the coin |

## Should you turn it on?

Leave it off and a link opens the coin for you to pick the amount, one extra tap. Turn it on for calls where seconds matter and you already trust the source.

<Warning>
  With group links on, a tap is a trade. Set an amount of your own, one you'd be relaxed about losing to a mis-tap. It wins over the card: a \$500 buy link spends your \$10. **Card Amount** trusts the size the poster chose, so only use it in a room you trust.
</Warning>
