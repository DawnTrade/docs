This setting is about **links you tap**, not links you post. It decides whether a one-tap trade in a group trades for you immediately, or just opens the coin.

`/settings` → **Group Links**. It belongs to the profile, and it is **off** by default.

## It has two parts

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **The toggle** | Turns group links on for this profile. Off, every link only opens the coin. |
| **The buy amount** | What a tapped buy link spends. |

The buy amount is a row of buttons on the same screen:

| Choice | What a tapped buy link spends |
| --- | --- |
| **Card Amount** | Whatever the button on the card says, between \$1 and \$10,000 — the default. Sell links open the sell panel rather than selling. |
| Your **buy presets** | The same five amounts as your token panel — see [Presets](/settings/presets). |
| **Custom** | Any figure from **\$1** to **\$10,000**. |

<Note>
  Set an amount of your own and it wins over the card: a \$500 buy link spends your \$10. Leave it on **Card Amount** and you are trusting the size the poster chose, so only do that in a room you trust.
</Note>

## What a tap does

|  | With group links armed | With them off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **A buy link** | Buys **your** amount, from this profile's wallet | Opens the coin's panel |
| **A sell link** | Sells the share the link names of what you hold — once you have an amount of your own set. On Card Amount it opens the sell panel. | Opens the coin's panel |

<Tip>
  With an amount of your own set, the size on the link is only what the card advertises — **your amount is what buys**, and you cannot be talked into a larger trade than you set for yourself. On **Card Amount** the link's size is the trade, capped at \$10,000.
</Tip>

## What it does not do

<Note>
  Only you are ever affected by a link **you** tap.

  Nobody can trade for you without your tap, and nothing happens to anyone who does not tap. There is no setting anywhere in Dawn that lets one person trade from another person's wallet.
</Note>

## Should you turn it on?

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Leave it off" icon="shield-halved" horizontal>
    The sensible default. You still get one-tap access to the coin; you just approve the amount. Costs you one extra tap.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Turn it on" icon="bolt" horizontal>
    For calls where seconds matter and you already trust the source. Set the buy amount to something small first.
  </Card>
</Columns>

<Warning>
  With this armed, a tap is a trade. In a fast-moving group that is exactly the point, and it is also how people buy things they did not mean to buy. Set the buy amount to something you would be relaxed about losing to a mis-tap.
</Warning>
