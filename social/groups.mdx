Every trade started from your group's cards is credited to your [referral code](/rewards/referrals).

## Set it up

Tap **Add Dawn to Group** on the home screen or a coin's Share screen, and pick your group. Dawn joins as an admin. Whoever adds it becomes the group's owner in Dawn, and their referral code goes on its cards.

## Admin commands

Run these in the group.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/setup` | Register the group, or take it over as an admin if Dawn is already there |
| `/amounts 5 10 25 50` | Set the buy buttons, one to four amounts. The default is **\$5 · \$10 · \$25 · \$50**. |
| `/compact on` | Shorten the card, worth it in a busy chat. `/compact off` restores it. |

## What members get

A tap buys from the member's own Dawn wallet, in their own private chat with Dawn, following their own [Group links](/social/group-links) setting. The group never sees anyone's balance. Someone new to Dawn is taken through starting the bot first.

<Tip>
  Pick button amounts that suit the room, and say in the group description that the cards carry your referral code.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  A card is not a recommendation. Anyone can paste any address, including a scam, and Dawn posts a card for it like any other.
</Warning>
