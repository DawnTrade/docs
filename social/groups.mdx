Add Dawn to a Telegram group and it watches for contract addresses. When someone posts one, Dawn replies with the coin's market card and a row of buy buttons.

Every trade started from your group is attributed to your [referral code](/rewards/referrals), so the group earns for you as it trades.

## Set it up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Add the bot">
    From the Rewards or Share screen, use the **add to group** link. Telegram asks which group, and adds Dawn as an admin so it can read the messages people post.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the owner">
    Whoever adds the bot becomes the group's owner in Dawn, and their referral code is the one attached to its cards.

    If the bot is already there and you want to take it over as an admin, run `/setup` in the group.

    <Note>
      A forwarded invite link cannot hand your group to somebody else.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Post an address">
    Dawn replies with the coin's card: name, chain, contract, market data and stats, with buy buttons underneath.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Admin commands

Run these **in the group**.

| Command | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `/setup` | Register the group, or take it over as an admin |
| `/amounts 5 10 25 50` | Set the buy buttons. One to four amounts |
| `/compact on` | Shorten the card. `/compact off` restores the full one |

The default buttons are **\$5 · \$10 · \$25 · \$50**.

Pick amounts that suit the room. Buttons that are too big for your members do not get tapped.

## What members get

Whoever taps a button buys **from their own Dawn wallet, in their own private chat with Dawn**. The trade never happens in the group, and the group never sees anyone's balance.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Whose money | The tapper's own |
| How much | Never more than **their own** [auto-buy amount](/settings/overview#auto-buy) |
| Someone who has never used Dawn | Is taken through starting the bot first |
| Someone who does not tap | Nothing happens at all |
| Someone with [group links](/social/group-links) off | The coin simply opens for them to decide |

<Note>
  The buy amounts you set on the card are a suggestion, not a power. Each member's own cap always wins.
</Note>

## Being a good host

- **Keep the card useful.** `/compact on` is worth it in a busy chat where full cards flood the feed.
- **Every card carries your referral code.** Members should know that — say so in the group description.
- **Dawn posts what it is asked to.** It does not check whether a coin is worth buying, and a card is not an endorsement by you or by Dawn.

<Warning>
  A card appearing in your group is not a recommendation. Anyone can paste any address, including a scam, and the card will render for it exactly as it would for anything else.
</Warning>
