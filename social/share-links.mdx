Every token panel has a 🔗 **Share** button. It turns the coin into something you can post, with your [referral link](/rewards/referrals) built in.

Anyone who trades through it becomes your referral, and you earn a share of the fees they pay from then on — not just on that trade.

## The three kinds

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Token link" icon="link">
    Opens the coin's panel in Dawn. The reader picks their own amount.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Buy link" icon="cart-shopping">
    You pick the amount. One tap buys it from the tapper's own wallet.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Sell link" icon="rotate">
    You pick the share. One tap sells that much of whatever the tapper holds.
  </Card>
</Columns>

Buy and sell links are the **Quick Trade** options on the Share screen. Choose the amount or the percentage, and Dawn gives you a card to post.

## Posting it

**↗️ Share** lets you pick any chat and posts the card straight into it. The bot does not need to be a member of that chat for this to work.

You can also copy the link and paste it anywhere at all — X, a blog, a DM.

To have Dawn post cards **automatically** whenever an address appears in your own group, add the bot there instead: see [Dawn in your group](/social/groups).

## What happens when someone taps

This is the part worth being precise about, because it sounds alarming and is not.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Whose money | **Theirs.** The trade comes from the tapper's own Dawn wallet. |
| Whose decision | **Theirs.** Nothing happens to anyone who does not tap. |
| Where it happens | In **their** private chat with Dawn, not in the group. |
| How much | **Their own** [group link buy amount](/social/group-links) if they set one; otherwise the size on your card. |
| If they have it off | The link just opens the coin for them to decide. |

A tapper who has [Group links](/social/group-links) switched off — which is the default — sees the coin's panel and nothing more. A tapper who has them on but has not set an amount of their own buys what your card says, up to \$10,000.

<Note>
  You cannot spend anyone else's money with a share link. The size you choose is what the card advertises; what actually gets spent is the amount that person set for themselves, or the card's size only if they chose to trust it.
</Note>

## Making them work

- **Post the coin, not the link.** A card with a chart and a price gets tapped; a bare referral URL does not.
- **Size the buy for the room.** A \$5 buy link converts far better than a \$500 one.
- **Use [separate codes](/rewards/codes)** for different places, so you can see which is actually working.

<Warning>
  You earn when the people you bring in trade. Pushing a coin you hold into a group you are also trading in is a conflict of interest, and people notice. Say what you hold.
</Warning>
