Tap 🔗 **Share** on any token panel. Anyone who trades through your link becomes your [referral](/rewards/referrals), and you earn a share of the fees they pay from then on, not just on that trade.

## The three kinds

| Link | What a tap does |
| --- | --- |
| **Token link** | Opens the coin's panel in Dawn. The reader picks their own amount. |
| **Buy link** | A one-tap buy of the amount you pick, from the tapper's own wallet. |
| **Sell link** | A one-tap sell of the share you pick, of whatever the tapper holds. |

<Info>
  A buy or sell link only trades for someone who has [Group links](/social/group-links) on (off by default). A buy spends the amount they set there, or your card's amount if they chose that. Otherwise the link just opens the coin for them.
</Info>

## Posting it

**↗️ Share** posts the card into any chat you pick, even one Dawn isn't in. You can also copy the link and paste it anywhere: X, a blog, a DM.

<Tip>
  To have Dawn post a card whenever an address appears in your own group, add it there: see [Dawn in your group](/social/groups).
</Tip>

## Making them work

- **Post the coin, not the link.** A card with a chart and a price gets tapped; a bare referral URL does not.
- **Size the buy for the room.** A \$5 buy link converts far better than a \$500 one.
- **Use [separate codes](/rewards/codes)** for different places, so you can see which one works.

<Warning>
  Pushing a coin you hold into a group you're also trading in is a conflict of interest, and people notice. Say what you hold.
</Warning>
