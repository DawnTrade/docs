Open a coin, tap **⚙️ Automations**, then 🪜 **Ladder**, or send `/ladder <address>`. Each rung is its own limit order, waiting for its own market cap.

## Build one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set both ends of the range">
    Send a market cap for each end, in either order.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Choose how many rungs">
    **4** by default, up to 10. More rungs give a smoother average, but each rung is its own trade with its own fee.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the total">
    A **total in dollars** for a buy ladder, or a **percentage of the position** for a sell ladder. It's split evenly between the rungs: a \$400 ladder over 4 rungs buys \$100 at each.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the expiry">
    **24 hours** unless you change it. See [Expiry](/orders/overview#expiry).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Place it">
    Tap **✅ Place buy ladder** or **✅ Place sell ladder**. **🗑 Cancel ladder** on Dawn's reply removes every rung that hasn't fired, all at once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## How the rungs are spaced

Evenly by **percentage move**, not by dollars. Across \$250k to \$2m, each rung is the same percentage step above the last, so your money is spread evenly over the move.

## Buy ladder or sell ladder

- **Buying:** set the range **below** the current cap to average into a fall, or **above** it to add into strength.
- **Selling:** set the range above the current cap to take profit in stages. Each rung sells its slice of the position.

Rungs fire independently. If the market runs through the whole range, several can fire close together; a rung the price never reaches sits until expiry.

## Ladder or DCA?

| | Triggered by |
| --- | --- |
| **Ladder** | **Price.** Rungs wait for market caps, and may never fire. Use it when you have a range in mind. |
| [**DCA**](/orders/dca) | **Time.** Buys happen on a schedule whatever the price does. Use it to build a position steadily. |
