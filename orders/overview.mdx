An automated order waits for a condition and then trades for you. It keeps running when you close Telegram, switch profile, or go to sleep.

## Which one do I want?

| I want to… | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Buy if it drops to a level | [Limit order](/orders/limit) |
| Buy if it breaks up through a level | [Limit order](/orders/limit) |
| Spread a buy across a range instead of one price | [Ladder](/orders/ladder) |
| Buy a fixed amount repeatedly over time | [DCA](/orders/dca) |
| Split a big buy or sell into small trades over time | [TWAP](/orders/twap) |
| Sell automatically once I am up | [Take profit](/orders/tp-sl) |
| Cap how much I can lose | [Stop loss](/orders/tp-sl) |
| Sell in stages on the way up | [TP levels](/orders/tp-sl#several-levels) or a [sell ladder](/orders/ladder) |
| Just be told when a coin reaches a level | [Price alert](/alerts/price) |
| Do what another wallet does, as it does it | [Copytrade](/copytrade/overview) |

## Targets are market caps

Levels in Dawn are usually a **market cap**, not a token price. Shorthand works: `250k`, `1.5m`, `$2m`.

<Info>
  A **\$1M target** means "when this coin is valued at \$1 million". It is not an instruction to trade \$1 million.
</Info>

## Order sizes

A buy spends a **dollar amount**. A sell sells a **percentage of what you hold**.

## Expiry

Limit orders and ladders expire after **24 hours** unless you pick another time, from 5 minutes to 30 days. `never` keeps an order until it fills or you cancel it.

Take-profit and stop-loss levels don't follow that schedule; they stay with the position.

## What a trigger does and does not promise

When its level is reached, an order becomes a **market trade** with your slippage and settings, and fills at whatever the market gives at that moment.

<Warning>
  A target is not a guaranteed price. On a thin coin the price can move a lot between the trigger and the fill, and a brief spike can trigger an order and be gone by the time it executes.
</Warning>

## Before you place several

- **Nothing is reserved.** Two \$100 limit buys need \$200 on your balance when they fire, not when you place them.
- **Orders belong to a wallet.** An order acts only on the holding of the wallet it was placed from.

See and cancel everything you have armed in [Managing orders](/orders/manage).
