A **take profit** sells after a rise, a **stop loss** after a fall. Both sit on a position you already hold.

Open the coin from your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) and choose **TP/SL**, or **Edit TP/SL** from `/orders`.

## Setting a level

Write a level as:

- A **percent** or **multiple** of your entry: `+100%`, `-30%`, `2x`
- A **market cap**: `$2m`, `450k`
- A **price**: `0.0034`

<Tip>
  A tighter stop protects more capital and gets knocked out more often. On a coin that routinely moves 30% in an hour, a −6% stop will be hit by noise rather than by a trend.
</Tip>

## Several levels

A side can hold **up to 5 levels**, each selling its own share. Enter them comma separated, like `+60%, +120%, +250%`: each sells an equal share, and the last sells whatever is left. To size them yourself, add the share after a colon: `+60%:30, +120%:30, +250%:40`.

A level set to 100% sells everything remaining at that point. So 50% at one level and 100% at the next sells half, then the rest.

## Defaults for every new position

Set exits once for the profile: `/settings` → **Stop Loss & Take Profit**. Every new position then gets those levels, and you can still change any position from its own page.

Defaults take only a percent or a multiple of your entry.

<Tip>
  A default stop loss is the single most useful setting in Dawn: it sells for you whether or not you are awake.
</Tip>

## Slippage on a stop

Stops sell with their own slippage, separate from your trading slippage: **20%** by default. Change it with **Stop slippage** in your stop-loss defaults.

Higher gets you out of a falling coin more reliably, at a worse price. Lower protects the price and risks not filling at all.

## What happens when one fires

Dawn messages you when a level fills, fails, expires or is paused. See [Notifications](/orders/manage#notifications).

<Warning>
  A stop loss is not a floor. It sells at whatever the market gives once the level is reached. A coin that gaps down, loses its liquidity, or blocks selling can leave you far below your level, or unable to exit at all.

  A stop that needs more gas than your [Gas Surplus](/settings/guardrails#gas-surplus) allows will not fire. If you rely on stops, set a surplus limit.
</Warning>
