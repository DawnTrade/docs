Send `/orders`, or tap **📋 Limit Orders** on the home screen. Everything armed is listed here by coin: limit buys and sells, ladders, DCA plans, running TWAPs, and the take-profit and stop-loss levels on your positions.

<Info>
  [Price alerts](/alerts/price) have their own list: **🔔 Alerts** → **📈 Price** on the home screen, or `/alerts`.
</Info>

## Changing a limit buy

Tap **✏️ Edit Orders** on the coin's limit panel, or **Edit** beside the orders line on the coin's panel. **Edit** on an order loads it back into the limit panel, where you can change its level, amount or expiry.

Limit sells are take-profit and stop-loss levels: change those with **Edit TP/SL**.

## Cancelling

Each order has its own **Cancel**, and DCA plans and TWAPs their own **Stop**. **🗑 Cancel All** clears everything on the page except DCA plans and TWAPs.

Cancelling never undoes something that already happened. A ladder that has filled two rungs keeps those two positions.

## Notifications

Dawn messages you when an order:

- **Fills**
- **Fails**
- **Expires** before its level is reached
- **Is paused**: the coin has no live price feed yet. It is still armed, and fires as normal once the feed is back.

<Note>
  Failed and expired orders do not retry themselves. If the protection mattered, put it back.
</Note>

## Why an order has not fired

- **The level has not been reached.** The common case. Compare the target with the coin's current market cap on its panel.
- **The price gapped through it.** If the market jumped past your level by more than your [slippage](/trading/execution#slippage) allows, Dawn does not fill into that gap. The order stays armed and waits for the price to come back into range.
- **You are in another profile.** The list only shows the wallet and profile you are in. Switch profile from the home screen to check the others.

<Warning>
  Closing a message, switching profile, archiving a wallet or closing Telegram cancels nothing. Before you step away from a coin, check `/orders` in **every profile**: a forgotten DCA plan keeps buying, and a forgotten limit buy keeps waiting.
</Warning>
