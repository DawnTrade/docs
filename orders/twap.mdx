---
title: "TWAP"
description: "Split a big buy or sell into small trades spread over time, so it moves the price less."
icon: "hourglass-half"
---

Open a coin, tap **⚙️ Automations**, then **⏲ TWAP**. It works on both sides of the panel: a buy spends dollars, a sell sells a share of what you hold.

## Start one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the amount">
    The total for the whole TWAP. A sell works from what you hold when you start it; at 100%, the last slice sells whatever is left.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick how long">
    Tap **Duration**: anything from 5 minutes to 24 hours. It starts on **30m**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose what to randomize">
    Optional. **Random** can vary when each slice goes (**Time**), how big each slice is (**Size**, same total), or both.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start it">
    Tap **✅ Start TWAP**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Notifications

Dawn posts a card for the TWAP, a short message for each slice that fills, and a summary when it finishes or stops on its own.

If a slice doesn't go through, its amount rides on the next one. Three failed slices in a row stop the TWAP.

## Stopping it

Tap **⏹ Stop TWAP** on its card, or **Stop** beside it in `/orders` or on the coin's ⏲ TWAP panel. Slices already done stay done.

## Fees

Each slice pays the [trading fee](/fees#the-trading-fee) like any other trade, its minimum included, so a small slice pays more than the rate.

## Limits

- Each slice is at least **\$1**, so the smallest TWAP is **\$2**, or **\$2.50** with Size randomized. An Ethereum buy slice is at least **\$5**.
- Up to **5** running at once per wallet.
