title: "DCA"
description: "Buy the same amount on a repeating schedule, whatever the price does."
icon: "repeat"
---

Open a coin, tap **⚙️ Automations**, then 🔁 **DCA**, or send `/dca <address>`.

## Set up a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Amount per buy">
    What each buy spends, not the total for the plan.
  </Step>

  <Step title="How often">
    From every **15 minutes** to every **7 days**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="How many buys">
    Up to **100**. The plan ends once it has made them all.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Start it">
    Tap **✅ Start DCA**. The first buy goes out within a minute, not after the first interval.
  </Step>
</Steps>

A plan spending \$25 every 6 hours for 20 buys commits **\$500 over five days**. You can run up to **5** plans at a time.

## Notifications

Dawn messages you when each buy goes through, is missed or fails, and when the plan finishes or stops.

## When a plan stops early

**Three failed buys in a row end the plan**, and nothing more is spent.

DCA reserves nothing: each buy takes from your balance when its turn comes, and a buy with too little USDC behind it fails. If a plan matters to you, keep enough on your balance to cover the buys that are left.

## Stopping a plan

Send `/orders` and tap **⏹ Stop DCA** for its coin. Stopping ends the buys that are left; it doesn't sell what the plan has already bought.
