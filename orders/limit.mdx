Open a coin, tap **⚙️ Automations**, then 📋 **Limit Order**. You can also place one in a single message: see [Place one with a command](#place-one-with-a-command).

## Place a limit buy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the market cap">
    Tap a trigger button for a move from the market cap now, or tap **Mcap** and send a level. Below the current cap, it buys the dip; above it, the breakout.

    <Tip>
      You can change the trigger buttons in [Presets](/settings/presets#limit-order-triggers).
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the spend">
    What the order spends **when it fires**. Nothing leaves your balance until then. Minimum **\$1**, or **\$5** on Ethereum.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the expiry">
    **24 hours** unless you change it. See [Expiry](/orders/overview#expiry).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create it">
    Tap **✅ Create Limit Order**. The coin's panel then lists it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can have up to **5** limit buys per coin on each wallet.

## Place a limit sell

The same flow from the **Selling** side of the panel, sized as a **percentage of what you hold** instead of dollars.

A limit sell is the manual version of a take profit. It can also be made [trailing](/orders/tp-sl#trailing-take-profit-and-trailing-stop). For several exits, or exits attached to the position, use [TP/SL](/orders/tp-sl).

## Place one with a command

`/limit` and a coin's address opens the limit panel; reply `/limit` to a coin's panel to open it on that panel's side. Add a trigger and an amount to place the order straight away:

| Message | What it places |
| --- | --- |
| `/limit CA 2x 50` | A \$50 buy when the market cap doubles |
| `/limit CA -30% 50` | A \$50 buy on a 30% dip |
| `/limit CA 2x 25%` | A sell of a quarter of your position at 2x |
| `/limit CA 200k 100 7d` | A \$100 buy at a \$200K market cap, open for 7 days |

- **Trigger**: a move from the market cap now (`2x`, `+50%`, `-30%`), a market cap (`200k`, `2.5m`), or a price (`$0.002`). A number with no `k`, `m` or `b` is read as a price, so write `5k` for a \$5,000 market cap.
- **Amount**: a dollar figure buys; a percent sells that share of what you hold.
- **Expiry**: buys only, 24 hours if you leave it out. A sell stays until it fills or you cancel it.

Replying to a coin's panel, leave out the address.

## After it fires

Dawn messages you like a manual trade, and the position appears in your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio). If it fails, your USDC stays on your balance.

<Info>
  If the price jumps past your level by more than your slippage allows, the order waits instead of filling into the gap. See [Why an order has not fired](/orders/manage#why-an-order-has-not-fired).
</Info>

## Editing and cancelling

Tap **✏️ Edit Orders** on the coin's limit panel to change or cancel its limit buys, or send `/orders` to see everything at once. See [Managing orders](/orders/manage).
