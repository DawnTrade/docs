A limit order waits for a market cap and then fires as a market trade.

Open a coin, tap **⚙️ Automations**, then 📋 **Limit Order**. The panel's buttons become the order you are building. You can also place one in a single message — see [Place one with a command](#place-one-with-a-command).

## Place a limit buy

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the market cap">
    Tap a trigger button to aim at a move from the market cap right now: **↑ 15% · ↑ 40% · ↑ 75% · ↑ 100%** above it, or **↓ 20% · ↓ 50% · ↓ 75%** below. The **Mcap** button then shows the level it lands on.

    Or tap **Mcap** and send a level. The prompt shows the coin's cap right now so you have something to aim from. A market cap like `110k`, `2.5m` or `\$2m` works, and so does a move like `+50%`, `2x` or `-30%`.

    | Where you put it | What you get |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Below** the current cap | A dip buy — it fires if the coin falls to your level |
    | **Above** the current cap | A breakout buy — it fires if the coin rises to it |

    The trigger buttons are yours to change in [Presets](/settings/presets#limit-order-triggers).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the spend">
    Tap a dollar button, or type an amount like `12.50`. Minimum **\$1**.

    This is what the order spends **when it fires**, not now. Nothing leaves your balance until then.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set the expiry">
    **24 hours** by default. Choose 1h, 24h, 3d, 7d or `never`, or type something like `36h`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create it">
    **✅ Create Limit Order** appears once the level and the amount are both set. The coin's panel then [lists it](/trading/token-panel#the-buttons) — its size and the market cap it is waiting for — and the **⚙️ Automations** button carries a count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Place a limit sell

The same flow from the **Selling** side of the panel. The one difference is the size: you choose a **percentage of what you hold**, not a dollar amount. The sell side's trigger buttons start at **↑ 1.5x · ↑ 2x · ↑ 5x · ↑ 10x**, and **↓ 20% · ↓ 50% · ↓ 75%**.

A limit sell is the manual version of a take-profit. If you want several exits, or you want them attached to the position rather than to a target you chose today, use [TP/SL](/orders/tp-sl) instead.

## Place one with a command

`/limit` opens the limit panel, or places a whole order in one message.

| Send | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `/limit` and a coin's address | Opens the limit panel for that coin |
| `/limit` as a reply to a coin's panel | Opens the limit panel on the side that panel is on |
| `/limit`, an address, a trigger and an amount | Places the order straight away |

| Message | What it places |
| --- | --- |
| `/limit CA 2x 50` | A \$50 buy when the market cap doubles |
| `/limit CA -30% 50` | A \$50 buy on a 30% dip |
| `/limit CA 2x 25%` | A sell of a quarter of your position at 2x |
| `/limit CA -40% 100%` | A sell of everything, 40% down |
| `/limit CA 200k 100 7d` | A \$100 buy at a \$200K market cap, open for 7 days |

- **Trigger** — a move from the market cap now (`2x`, `+50%`, `-30%`), a market cap (`200k`, `2.5m`), or a price (`$0.002`). A number with no `k`, `m` or `b` is read as a price, so write `5k` for a \$5,000 market cap.
- **Amount** — a dollar figure buys; a percent sells that share of what you hold.
- **Expiry** — buys only: `45m`, `36h`, `7d` or `never`. Leave it out for 24 hours. A sell stays until it fills or you cancel it.

Replying to a coin's panel, leave out the address. Dawn confirms with **✅ Limit buy placed** or **✅ Limit sell placed** and the level it fires at, with **🗑 Cancel Order** on the card.

## When the price gaps past your level

Markets jump. If a coin blows straight through your level by more than your [slippage](/trading/execution#slippage) allows, Dawn **does not** fire the order into that gap — filling there would mean paying far more than you asked for.

The order stays armed and waits for the price to come back into range.

<Note>
  This is why an order can look like it "should have fired" and still be active. The level was passed, but only at a price you had already said no to.
</Note>

## Limits

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Limit buys per coin, per wallet | **5** |
| Minimum spend | **\$1** |
| Expiry | 5 minutes to 30 days, or never |

## After it fires

You get a card in the chat exactly like a manual trade, and the position appears in your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio).

If it fails, the card says why and your USDC stays on your balance. A failed order does not automatically retry — place a new one if you still want it.

## Editing and cancelling

Tap **✏️ Edit Orders** on the coin's limit panel to change or cancel its limit buys, or send `/orders` to see everything at once. See [Managing orders](/orders/manage).
