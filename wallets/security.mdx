## What protects your funds

- **Dawn doesn't hold your keys.** [Privy](https://privy.io) creates and holds them, and you can [export any key](/wallets/import-export#export-a-private-key) at any time.
- **You approve every send and withdrawal** yourself, in the wallet app — see [Approve it in the wallet app](/funds/withdraw#approve-it-in-the-wallet-app).
- [**Two-factor authentication**](/wallets/two-factor) puts a second check on sends, withdrawals and key exports. Turn it on from `/wallets` → **🔐 2FA**.
- **Every trade is checked against its quote.** If it has drifted from what you confirmed, it fails instead of filling badly.

## Secure your Telegram

Your Telegram account is your Dawn login. Turn on **Two-Step Verification** in Telegram: Settings → Privacy and Security → Two-Step Verification.

## What Dawn will never ask for

- Your private key. Importing happens encrypted, inside the app.
- A seed phrase. Dawn wallets don't have one.
- A Telegram login code.
- A 2FA code. Privy asks for it in its own window, never in a chat.
- A payment to unlock funds. Withdrawals never need a deposit first.

Support is reached through [@DawnContactBot](https://t.me/DawnContactBot) and asks for none of these. Treat anyone who does as an impostor, even an account using the Dawn name and logo.

<Tip>
  Leaving Dawn? Export your keys first, and the wallets are yours without Dawn. [Archiving](/wallets/wallets#archive-and-restore) a wallet only takes it off your list.
</Tip>

## Risks Dawn cannot remove

<Warning>
  A coin's contract can let its deployer mint more supply, freeze your balance, block selling, or raise its tax after you buy. Many memecoins are built this way. A clean token panel means nothing was detected, not that nothing is there.
</Warning>
