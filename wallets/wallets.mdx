A Dawn wallet is a **pair of addresses**, one EVM and one Solana, with its own balance, positions and orders. It gets a NEAR address too the first time it [trades on NEAR](/trading/near). Your first one is made for you when you send `/start`.

Open your wallets with `/wallets`, or **Wallets** on the home screen.

## Your default wallet

The wallet marked ⭐ **Default** is the one in use: trades, orders and deposits go there unless you pick another. Tap **Set as default** on another wallet to switch.

<Info>
  **Ready** and **Portfolio** on the home screen count every wallet in the [profile](/wallets/profiles) you're in.
</Info>

## Create a wallet

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the profile">
    A wallet trades with its profile's settings, so switch to the right [profile](/wallets/profiles) first.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add it">
    In `/wallets`, tap **New wallet**. Tap **Set as default** on it to trade from it.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fund it">
    A new wallet starts empty. [Deposit](/funds/deposit) into it, or [send](/funds/withdraw#send-to-your-own-wallets) USDC from another wallet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Each profile holds up to **5 wallets**, so **25** across 5 profiles.

<Tip>
  With several wallets in a profile, you can buy or sell a coin from all of them in one go — see [Multi-wallet trading](/trading/multi-wallet).
</Tip>

## Rename a wallet

On the wallet's **Manage** page, tap **Rename**.

## Archive and restore

Archiving takes a wallet off your list without touching what's in it. Nothing is sold or sent, the keys stay with Privy, and its positions and history stay with it.

Restore it from **Archived wallets**. It comes back into its profile, but not as the wallet in use.

<Warning>
  Archiving does not cancel orders already placed. Cancel anything you don't want to fire first — see [Managing orders](/orders/manage).
</Warning>

## Keys and 2FA

Dawn doesn't hold your private keys: [Privy](https://privy.io) does, and you can [export them](/wallets/import-export#export-a-private-key) at any time.

Turn on [two-factor authentication](/wallets/two-factor) from **🔐 2FA** under the list, to put a second check on sends and key exports.
