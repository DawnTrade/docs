A **profile** is a set of trading settings shared by the wallets in it. Balances, positions, orders and history belong to each wallet; the profile decides how they trade.

Open it with **Switch profile** on the home screen. You can have up to **5** profiles.

<Accordion title="Example: two profiles">
  A careful one with low slippage, auto-buy off and a stop loss on every position by default. A fast one with Degen mode on, auto-buy armed at a fixed size and bigger buy buttons.
</Accordion>

## What belongs to the profile

- Slippage, Auto Buy and its amount, MEV protection, Gas preset, Fees in PnL — [Trading settings](/settings/overview#trading)
- Degen Mode, Auto-Adjust, Gas Surplus — [Guardrails](/settings/guardrails)
- Default Stop Loss and Take Profit — [Auto sell defaults](/settings/overview#auto-sell)
- Buy and sell buttons — [Presets](/settings/presets)
- [Group links](/social/group-links)

## Create a profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Name it">
    Tap **New profile** and send a name.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Starting settings">
    Copy the settings of the profile you were just using, or start from the defaults. Only settings are copied, not wallets, funds or positions.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Fund its wallet">
    A new profile comes with a fresh, empty wallet. [Deposit](/funds/deposit) into it, or [send](/funds/withdraw#send-to-your-own-wallets) USDC from another profile.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Switch profiles

Tap **Switch profile** on the home screen, then the profile you want. The home screen, token panel and portfolio follow the profile you're in.

<Warning>
  Switching doesn't stop automation. Stop losses, take profits, limit orders and DCA plans keep running in every profile. Check `/orders` in each one before you assume nothing is armed.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  A coin missing from your portfolio is almost always in another profile, or another wallet in this one. See [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
</Tip>
