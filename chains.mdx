## How one balance reaches nine chains

Your balance is USDC on Solana. A Solana coin is bought from it directly. For a coin on any other chain, Dawn moves the value across as part of the trade: no bridging step for you, and no second balance to keep. The cost of that route is [inside the quote](/fees#other-costs).

You can deposit from more chains than Dawn trades on — see [Deposit](/funds/deposit) and [Withdraw and send](/funds/withdraw).

## Naming the chain yourself

The same `0x…` address can exist on more than one EVM chain, and Dawn asks which one you mean when it cannot tell. To skip the question, put the chain in front of the address:

| Type | For |
| --- | --- |
| `sol <address>` | Solana |
| `bnb 0x…` | BNB Chain |
| `rh 0x…` | Robinhood Chain |
| `base 0x…` | Base |
| `arc 0x…` | Arc |
| `ink 0x…` | Ink |
| `hype 0x…` | HyperEVM |
| `eth 0x…` | Ethereum |
| `near <address>` | NEAR |
