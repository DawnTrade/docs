Dawn trades on six networks. You do not choose one — the coin's contract address decides, and Dawn reaches it from the same balance.

| Chain | Coin | Explorer |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🟣 **Solana** | SOL | [Solscan](https://solscan.io) |
| 🟡 **BNB Chain** | BNB | [BscScan](https://bscscan.com) |
| 🟢 **Robinhood Chain** | ETH | [Etherscan](https://robin.etherscan.io) |
| 🔵 **Base** | ETH | [BaseScan](https://basescan.org) |
| ⚪ **Arc** | USDC | [Arc Explorer](https://explorer.arc.io) |
| 🟦 **Ink** | ETH | [Ink Explorer](https://explorer.inkonchain.com) |

## Naming the chain yourself

Most of the time you paste an address and Dawn works out the rest. Two cases need help:

- **The same address exists on several chains.** EVM addresses can be identical across BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Base, Arc and Ink. Dawn asks which one you meant.
- **Dawn cannot find the coin.** Put the chain in front of the address to skip the search:

```text
bnb 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
rh 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
base 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
arc 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
ink 0x0000000000000000000000000000000000000000
```

Solana addresses are unambiguous, so they never need a prefix.
