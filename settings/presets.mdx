Open `/settings` → **🎛 Presets**, then the set you want to change. Each [profile](/wallets/profiles) has its own.

## Buy amounts

Five dollar amounts, spent from your balance, in the order you set. The default is **\$1 · \$5 · \$10 · \$25 · \$50**.

<Tip>
  Keep the **leftmost** buy button small. It's the one nearest your thumb, and the one you'll hit by accident.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="Example setups">
  - **Small and fast**: \$1 · \$2 · \$5 · \$10 · \$25, for taking a lot of small shots at new launches.
  - **Fewer, bigger**: \$50 · \$100 · \$250 · \$500 · \$1000, when you're sizing properly and not scalping.
</Accordion>

## Sell amounts

Shares of what you hold, not dollar amounts. The default is **10% · 25% · 50% · 75% · 100%**. For taking profit in stages, `25 · 33 · 50 · 75 · 100` works better.

## Limit order triggers

Each trigger button on the [limit order panel](/orders/limit) is a move from the coin's market cap at the moment you tap it: **↑ 40%** on a coin at \$1M waits for \$1.4M. Limit buys and limit sells have their own set.

<Accordion title="Default triggers">
  | Set | Rises | Falls |
  | --- | --- | --- |
  | 🎯 **Limit Buy Triggers** | ↑ 15% · ↑ 40% · ↑ 75% · ↑ 100% | ↓ 20% · ↓ 50% · ↓ 75% |
  | 🎯 **Limit Sell Triggers** | ↑ 1.5x · ↑ 2x · ↑ 5x · ↑ 10x | ↓ 20% · ↓ 50% · ↓ 75% |
</Accordion>

<Info>
  Whatever your presets, **\$X** on the buy side and **X%** on the sell side take an exact amount, and **Mcap** on the limit order panel takes any level or move you type.
</Info>
