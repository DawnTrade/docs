The quick-pick buttons in Dawn are yours: what the buy and sell buttons trade, and where the limit order panel's trigger buttons aim.

`/settings` → **🎛 Presets**, then pick a set:

| Set | What it changes |
| --- | --- |
| 💵 **Buy Presets** | The dollar buttons for buying |
| 📉 **Sell Presets** | The share buttons for selling |
| 🎯 **Limit Buy Triggers** | The trigger buttons on a limit buy |
| 🎯 **Limit Sell Triggers** | The trigger buttons on a limit sell |

Tap a button in the set to change it.

## Buy amounts

Dollar amounts, spent from your balance. Five buttons, in the order you put them.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Default | **\$1 · \$5 · \$10 · \$25 · \$50** |
| Range | \$1 to \$10,000 |
| Decimals | Allowed — `2.50` works |

## Sell amounts

Shares of what you hold, not dollar figures.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Default | **10% · 25% · 50% · 75% · 100%** |
| Range | Whole percents, 1 to 100 |

Sells are percentages because your position size changes. A percentage stays meaningful after you have bought more or sold some; a dollar figure does not.

## Limit order triggers

The trigger buttons on the [limit order panel](/orders/limit) are moves from the coin's market cap at the moment you tap them. Tap **↑ 40%** on a coin at \$1M and the order waits for \$1.4M.

| Set | Rises | Falls |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Limit Buy Triggers** | ↑ 15% · ↑ 40% · ↑ 75% · ↑ 100% | ↓ 20% · ↓ 50% · ↓ 75% |
| 🎯 **Limit Sell Triggers** | ↑ 1.5x · ↑ 2x · ↑ 5x · ↑ 10x | ↓ 20% · ↓ 50% · ↓ 75% |

Those are the defaults. Tap one and send its new trigger:

| Type | Means |
| --- | --- |
| `+25%` | A quarter above the market cap then |
| `3x` | Three times it |
| `-40%` | 40% below it |

A plain `25%` counts as a rise.

## Making them fit how you trade

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Small and fast" icon="feather" horizontal>
    \$1 · \$2 · \$5 · \$10 · \$25 — for taking a lot of small shots at new launches.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fewer, bigger" icon="weight-hanging" horizontal>
    \$50 · \$100 · \$250 · \$500 · \$1000 — when you are sizing properly and not scalping.
  </Card>
</Columns>

On the sell side, `25 · 33 · 50 · 75 · 100` suits taking profit in stages better than the default does.

<Tip>
  Leave the **leftmost** buy button small. It is the one nearest your thumb and the one you will hit by accident.
</Tip>

## Resetting

**↺ Reset to defaults** on any set puts the original buttons back.

Presets belong to the [profile](/wallets/profiles), so each profile can have its own — and a new profile can copy them from an existing one.

## Beyond the presets

**\$x** on the buy side and **X%** on the sell side always let you type an exact amount, whatever your buttons are set to. On the limit order panel, **Mcap** takes any level or move you type.
