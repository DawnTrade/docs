The buttons on a token panel are yours. Five for buying, five for selling, in the order you put them.

`/settings` → **Buy Amounts** or **Sell Amounts**. Tap a button to change it.

## Buy amounts

Dollar amounts, spent from your balance.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Default | **\$1 · \$5 · \$10 · \$25 · \$50** |
| Range | \$1 to \$10,000 |
| Decimals | Allowed — `2.50` works |

## Sell amounts

Shares of what you hold, not dollar figures.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Default | **10% · 25% · 50% · 75% · 100%** |
| Range | Whole percents, 1 to 100 |

Sells are percentages because your position size changes. A percentage stays meaningful after you have bought more or sold some; a dollar figure does not.

## Making them fit how you trade

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Small and fast" icon="feather" horizontal>
    \$1 · \$2 · \$5 · \$10 · \$25 — for taking a lot of small shots at new launches.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Fewer, bigger" icon="weight-hanging" horizontal>
    \$50 · \$100 · \$250 · \$500 · \$1000 — when you are sizing properly and not scalping.
  </Card>
</Columns>

On the sell side, `25 · 33 · 50 · 75 · 100` suits taking profit in stages better than the default does.

<Tip>
  Leave the **leftmost** buy button small. It is the one nearest your thumb and the one you will hit by accident.
</Tip>

## Resetting

**Reset defaults** on either screen puts the original five back.

Presets belong to the [profile](/wallets/profiles), so each profile can have its own — and a new profile can copy them from an existing one.

## Beyond the presets

**\$x** on the buy side and **X%** on the sell side always let you type an exact amount, whatever your buttons are set to.
