Send `/settings`. Settings belong to the **profile you're in**: [each profile has its own](/wallets/profiles), and a new one can start as a copy of yours.

## Trading

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🎯 **Slippage** | **15%** | How far the price may move against you before a trade is refused. Stop losses, trailing sells and dev sells use it too. See [Execution](/trading/execution#slippage). |
| ⚡ **Auto Buy** | Off | Pasting an address buys a fixed amount right away. |
| 🛡 **MEV Protection** | **On** | Sends Solana swaps through a private lane so nobody can front-run you. See [Execution](/trading/execution#mev-protection). |
| ⛽️ **Gas** | Medium | How hard your trades bid to land. See [Execution](/trading/execution#gas-preset). |
| 💵 **Fees in PnL** | Off | Shows what a position has paid in fees beside its profit. PnL is net of fees either way. |

### Auto Buy

With Auto Buy on, **pasting a coin's address buys it** for the amount you set. With it off, pasting only opens the coin.

<Warning>
  This turns pasting into spending: forwarding a message, or pasting an address just to look at it, becomes a trade. Keep the amount to something you'd be relaxed about losing to a mis-tap.
</Warning>

## Guardrails

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔥 **Degen Mode** | Off | 100% slippage and no liquidity protection on buys |
| 🪄 **Auto-Adjust** | Off | Shrinks a buy you can't afford instead of refusing it |
| 💸 **Gas Surplus** | Off | How much extra gas a trade may charge you above Dawn's \$5 cap |

See [Guardrails](/settings/guardrails).

## Auto Sell

| Setting | Default |
| --- | --- |
| 📉 **Stop Loss** | Off |
| 📈 **Take Profit** | Off |
| 👨‍💻 **Dev Sell** | Off |

Exits set here go on **every new position** in the profile, as a percent like `-30%`, a multiple like `2x`, or a [trailing sell](/orders/tp-sl#trailing-take-profit-and-trailing-stop). You can still change any position's own afterwards. See [Take profit and stop loss](/orders/tp-sl#defaults-for-every-new-position).

<Tip>
  A default stop loss is the most valuable setting on this page. It's the difference between a bad trade costing you part of a position and all of it.
</Tip>

## Groups

| Setting | Default | What it changes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🔗 **Group Links** | Off | Whether tapping a one-tap trade in a chat trades right away, or just opens the coin |
| 💵 **Buy Amount** | Card Amount | What a tapped buy link spends |

See [Group links](/social/group-links).

## Presets

**🎛 Presets** sets your quick-pick buttons: the buy amounts and sell shares on every token panel, and the limit order triggers. See [Presets](/settings/presets).
