`/settings` → **Guardrails**. All three belong to the [profile](/wallets/profiles), and all three are **off** by default.

## Degen Mode

|  | Buys |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** | Use your slippage. A buy that would move the price more than **30%** is [declined, or asks first](/trading/execution#liquidity-protection). |
| **On** | **100% slippage** and **no liquidity protection**: a buy goes through however far it moves the price, and can fill a long way from the quote. |

Auto Buy and limit buys follow it too. **Sells always keep your normal slippage.** Switch it here, or with the **Degen** button on any token panel.

<Warning>
  Use it only when a fill at any price beats no fill, like a launch moving faster than any slippage can follow. On a thin pool it's how you buy the top of your own candle. It doesn't make a restricted token sellable, and it doesn't guarantee a fill.
</Warning>

## Auto-Adjust

On, a buy bigger than your balance is **reduced** to the largest buy your balance covers, and goes through. Off, it's refused and nothing is spent. Useful if you often trade close to your full balance.

## Gas Surplus

Dawn pays up to **\$5** of network gas on every trade. Gas Surplus is how much more a trade may take from your USDC when it needs more than that, up to **\$50**. Past your limit, or with it off, the trade is declined and nothing is spent.

<Warning>
  Take-profit and stop-loss sells follow the same limit: **a stop-loss that needs more gas than you allow will not fire.** If you rely on stops, set a limit rather than leaving it off, especially on a volatile chain where gas spikes just when you need to exit.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  **Careful**: Degen off, Auto-Adjust on, Gas Surplus \$2. **Fast**: Degen on, Auto-Adjust on, Gas Surplus \$10, which fills on almost anything, sometimes badly. Keep each in its own [profile](/wallets/profiles) rather than toggling between them.
</Tip>
