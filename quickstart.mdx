<Steps>
  <Step title="Start the bot">
    Open [Dawn](https://t.me/DawnTradeBot) in Telegram and tap **Start**, or send `/start`.

    ![Start Button](/images/start-button.png "Start Button")

    Dawn creates your first wallet: one Solana and one EVM address, secured by [Privy](https://privy.io). Nobody at Dawn holds your keys, and you can [export them](/wallets/import-export) whenever you like.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add USDC">
    Tap **Deposit** or send `/deposit`, then pick the chain you are sending **from** and the token you are sending.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/deposit-screen-initial.png"
        alt="Deposit Screen Initial"
        title="Deposit Screen Initial"
        style={{ width:"77%" }}
      />
    </Frame>

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/choose-funds.png"
        alt="Choose Funds"
        title="Choose Funds"
        style={{ width:"77%" }}
      />
    </Frame>

    Copy the address Dawn shows, and send from your wallet or exchange with the same token on the same network.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/address-and-minimum.png"
        alt="Address And Minimum"
        title="Address And Minimum"
        style={{ width:"67%" }}
      />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      Send only the token and chain the panel names, and at least its minimum. Anything else cannot be recovered.
    </Warning>

    <Tip>
      USDC on Solana is the most direct route: no fee and no conversion. See [Deposit](/funds/deposit) for the rest.
    </Tip>

    Wait for **Deposit received** before you trade.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open a coin">
    Paste the coin's contract address into the chat. Dawn replies with its [token panel](/trading/token-panel).

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/coin-information.png"
        alt="Coin Information"
        title="Coin Information"
        style={{ width:"65%" }}
      />
    </Frame>

    Check the address and the chain before you go further.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Buy">
    Tap a dollar amount. Start with one you would not mind losing; the minimum is **\$1**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/buy-buttons.png"
        alt="Buy Buttons"
        title="Buy Buttons"
      />
    </Frame>

    <Warning>
      Tapping an amount sends the trade. There is no second confirmation step.
    </Warning>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find it in your portfolio">
    Send `/portfolio`, or tap **Portfolio** on the home screen. Tap your coin to reopen its panel and buy more, sell, or set an order.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## If the buy doesn't go through

Your USDC is back on your balance, and the message says why. The usual fixes are in [Why a trade gets refused](/trading/buy-and-sell#why-a-trade-gets-refused).

Nothing happens when you paste? Check you pasted the contract address, not a link to a chart.
