The token panel is where trading happens. Paste a coin's contract address into the chat and it opens.

## Opening a coin

- **Contract address**: the exact way. Paste it and Dawn works out the chain.
- **Ticker**: send `$PEPE`, or `/search pepe`.
- **Chain prefix**: `bnb 0x…`, `base 0x…`, `hype 0x…` when an address is on more than one chain — [every prefix](/chains#naming-the-chain-yourself).

<Warning>
  Anyone can deploy a coin called PEPE. After a ticker search, check the address on the panel before you buy.
</Warning>

<Tip>
  To browse instead, tap **🔥 Trending** on the home screen or send `/trending` — see [Trending and search](/trading/trending).
</Tip>

## The market block

| Line | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **MC** | Market cap. Order and alert targets are usually set against this, not the token price. The arrow beside it is how far it has moved since you opened the panel. |
| **Liq** | How much money is in the coin's pool, and that as a share of its market cap. |
| **Price** | Price per token. `0.0₇5544` is `0.00000005544`: the small number counts the zeros after the point. |

<Tip>
  The percentage next to **Liq** is the number to look at. A coin with a \$2M cap and \$20k of liquidity is 1%: your own buy will move the price hard, and getting out is worse than getting in.
</Tip>

## The stats block

| Line | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Top 10** | The share of supply the ten biggest holders own between them. |
| **1h** buys · sells | Trades in the last hour: whether anyone is still trading it. |
| _prices as of … ago_ | The numbers are stale. Treat them as indicative. |

A high **Top 10** share means a handful of wallets can exit into your bid. A coin minutes old with few holders is the highest-risk thing on the screen.

## Risk warnings

Dawn checks Solana and EVM coins for traps that stop you selling: a sell that fails, a sell tax of half or more, or a Solana creator who can freeze, take or block your tokens. Buying a coin it flags asks you to confirm first. Selling never does.

<Info>
  [Auto Buy](/settings/overview#auto-buy) and a buy link tapped with [group links](/social/group-links) on open a flagged coin's panel instead of buying. [Copytrade](/copytrade/overview#notifications) skips it unless the task's [⚠️ Copy Risky Tokens](/copytrade/manage#filters) is on.
</Info>

## Coins still on a launchpad curve

On Solana (pump.fun) and Robinhood Chain (Pons), a coin that has not graduated to a regular pool shows:

| Line | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Bonding curve** | How far along the curve it is. At 100% it graduates to a regular pool. |
| **💸 Cashback coin** | This coin pays a share of every trade back to you. See [Pump.fun cashback](/rewards/pump-fun). |

You can trade these and set [orders](/orders/overview) on them like any other coin. A coin this new can briefly have no live price; its orders are marked **paused** meanwhile, but stay active.

## Your holding

Once you own some, the **Holding** block shows your position and PnL for the wallet in use, with a link to a [PnL banner](/trading/pnl-cards). With several wallets holding the coin, see [Multi-wallet trading](/trading/multi-wallet).

## The buttons

- **Amount buttons** send the trade the moment you tap: dollar amounts when buying, shares of your holding when selling. Change them in [Presets](/settings/presets).
- **⚙️ Automations** holds [limit orders](/orders/limit), [ladders](/orders/ladder), [DCA](/orders/dca), [TWAP](/orders/twap) and [price alerts](/alerts/price) for the coin, and on the Selling side, [trailing sells](/orders/tp-sl#trailing-take-profit-and-trailing-stop).
- **🔗 Share**, **👥 Multi-Wallet**, **Degen** and **📝 Thesis**: see [Share links](/social/share-links), [Multi-wallet trading](/trading/multi-wallet), [Degen Mode](/settings/guardrails#degen-mode) and [Top theses](#top-theses).

<Info>
  Take-profit and stop-loss levels aren't under Automations: open the coin from your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) and choose **TP/SL**, or **Edit TP/SL** in `/orders`. See [Take profit and stop loss](/orders/tp-sl).
</Info>

## Top theses

**📝 Thesis** shows what traders on fomo and pump.fun have written about the coin, with each author's own position in it: **💰** what they put in, and **🎒** what it is worth now, or **closed** once they sold. **⚠️ deployer** marks the coin's own creator.

To hear about new theses from traders you follow as they post them, see [Trader Alerts](/alerts/traders#theses).

## What the panel cannot tell you

<Danger>
  Market data describes a coin's market, not what its contract can do.

  A contract can let its deployer mint more supply, freeze balances, block selling entirely, or raise its tax after you have bought. Liquidity can be pulled in a single transaction. [Risk warnings](#risk-warnings) catch some of this before you buy, not all of it, and nothing on the panel warns of a change made later.

  Treat every panel as the beginning of your own check, not the end of it.
</Danger>
