Open a coin and tap **👥 Multi-Wallet** on its [panel](/trading/token-panel). **Buying ⇄** switches between buying and selling.

## Buy from several wallets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the wallets">
    Only wallets with USDC can buy. Deposit to an empty one first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the amount">
    Every picked wallet buys that amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  The amount is for **each** wallet, not a total split between them: buying from five wallets spends five times the amount.
</Info>

## ⚡ Bundle on Solana

On Solana, with two or more wallets picked, you can turn on **⚡ Bundle**: every picked wallet buys in one transaction, at the same price, or none of them do.

|  | ⚡ Bundle on | Bundle off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Entry price | The same for every wallet, and nothing can trade in between them | Each buy walks the price up for the next |
| If one fails | None of them buy | The others still buy |

Each wallet pays its own trading fee; Dawn covers the network fee, as on any buy.

On the EVM chains there is no bundle: the buys go out at the same moment, each on its own, so one can fail without the others and fills can land at slightly different prices.

## Sell from several wallets

Works like buying, over the wallets that hold the coin. Pick the wallets, then a share: every picked wallet sells that share of **its own** holding. Each sells on its own, so one can fail without the others.

## What you get back

- A bundle sends one message for all its wallets, naming any wallet left out and why.
- Otherwise each wallet's trade gets its own message, like a normal trade.

## Where the positions end up

Each wallet holds its own position, with its own cost basis and PnL. The [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) shows the wallet you're in; switch wallet, or use the filters, to see the rest.

Orders are per wallet too: a stop loss set from one wallet protects only that wallet's holding.
