Open a coin and tap **👥 Multi-Wallet** on its [panel](/trading/token-panel) to buy or sell from any one wallet, or from all of them at once.

<Info>
  The amount is for **each** wallet, not a total split between them: buying from five wallets spends five times the amount.
</Info>

## ⚡ Bundle on Solana

On Solana, **⚡ Bundle** makes **👥 All wallets** trade in one transaction. A single wallet always trades on its own.

|  | ⚡ Bundle on | Bundle off |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Price | The same for every wallet, and nothing can trade in between them | Each trade moves the price for the next |
| If one fails | None of them trade | The others still go through |

Each wallet pays its own trading fee; Dawn covers the network fee on a bundled buy.

On the EVM chains there is no bundle: the trades go out at the same moment, each on its own, so one can fail without the others and fills can land at slightly different prices.

## What you get back

- A bundle sends one message for all its wallets, naming any wallet left out and why.
- Otherwise each wallet's trade gets its own message, like a normal trade.

## Where the positions end up

Each wallet holds its own position, with its own cost basis and PnL. The [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) shows the wallet you're in; switch wallet, or use the filters, to see the rest.

Orders are per wallet too: a stop loss set from one wallet protects only that wallet's holding.
