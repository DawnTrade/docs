A profile can hold up to **5 wallets**. The multi-wallet panel trades the same coin from several of them without switching wallet between each one.

Open a coin and tap **👥 Multi-Wallet** on its panel. **Buying ⇄** switches between buying and selling.

## Buy from several wallets

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the wallets">
    Every wallet is a button showing its USDC. Tap one to pick it or drop it — picked wallets are highlighted. **✅ Select All** picks every wallet with USDC, and **✖ Clear** drops them all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap how much each one spends">
    Tap one of your dollar buttons, or **\$X** for your own. Every picked wallet buys that amount.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The page opens with the wallets you picked last time, or with every wallet that holds USDC, and remembers **⚡ Bundle** too. Each profile keeps its own. An empty wallet shows **\$0** and cannot be picked until you deposit to it.

<Info>
  Buying from five wallets spends five times the size you chose. The size applies to **each** picked wallet; it is not a total divided between them.
</Info>

## Buying from several at once

What "at once" means depends on the chain.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Solana">
    With two or more wallets picked, a **⚡ Bundle** toggle appears.

    **⚡ Bundle: On** — every picked wallet buys in one transaction, at the same price, or none of them do. Nobody can slip a trade in between your own wallets, and no wallet gets a worse entry than another.

    **⚡ Bundle: Off** — each wallet buys on its own, so one can fail without the others.

    Each wallet pays its own trading fee; Dawn covers the network fee, as on any buy.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="BNB, Robinhood, Base, Arc, Ink, HyperEVM">
    The buys go out at the same moment, each on its own. One can fail without affecting the others, and the fills can land at slightly different prices.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Why you would bundle

- **One entry price.** Five separate buys walk the price up; five bundled buys do not.
- **No sandwiching between your own wallets.** Nothing can get in between them.
- **All or nothing.** The wallets in the bundle either all get in or none do, so you are never left half-positioned.

### Why you might not

A bundle is all or nothing: if it fails, none of its wallets buy. With Bundle off, one wallet's failure never holds the others back.

## Selling from several wallets

The sell side lists each wallet that holds the coin, with your sell shares under it. Tap a share under a wallet to sell from that wallet; when two or more hold it, **🚀 All Wallets** sells the same share from each of them. Every tap is its own trade.

It also tells you how many of your profiles hold the coin, so you can see at a glance whether the rest of it is somewhere else.

## Reading the results

A bundle sends **one card** for the whole thing. Wallets that could not take part are named separately, with the reason.

Without ⚡ Bundle, and on every EVM chain, each wallet's buy gets its own card, exactly like a normal trade.

## Where the positions end up

Each wallet holds its own position with its own cost basis and its own PnL. The [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) shows the wallet you are in; switch wallet, or use the filters, to see the rest.

Orders are per-wallet too: a stop-loss set from one wallet protects that wallet's holding only.
