A profile can hold up to **5 wallets**. The multi-wallet panel trades the same coin from several of them without switching wallet between each one.

Open a coin and choose the multi-wallet view from the panel. Two buttons at the top switch between its two faces.

## Two ways to buy

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **💳 Per Wallet** | Every wallet is listed with its own row of sizes. Tap a size under a wallet and that wallet alone buys. |
| **🚀 Buy Together** | Every wallet is a tick. Tick the ones you want, then a size at the bottom, and they all buy at once. |

The first is for topping up one wallet. The second is for entering from several at the same moment.

## Buying from several at once

What "at once" means depends on the chain.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Solana">
    In **🚀 Buy Together**, a **⚡ Bundle** toggle appears.

    **Bundle on** lands the buys together: the **same block**, the **same price**, all or nothing. Nobody can slip a trade in between your own wallets, and no wallet gets a worse entry than another.

    Each wallet still pays its own trading fee and its own tip.

    **Bundle off** sends the buys at the same moment but each on its own, so one can fail without taking the others with it.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="BNB, Robinhood, Base, Arc, Ink">
    The buys go out at the same moment, each on its own. One can fail without affecting the others, and the fills can land at slightly different prices.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Why you would bundle

- **One entry price.** Five separate buys walk the price up; five bundled buys do not.
- **No sandwiching between your own wallets.** Nothing can get in between them.
- **All or nothing.** Either every wallet gets in or none does, so you are never left half-positioned.

### Why you might not

A bundle is all or nothing, so one wallet that cannot take part stops the whole thing. Sending them separately gets the rest of your wallets in regardless.

## Selling from several wallets

The sell panel works the same way, with **shares of each wallet's own holding** instead of dollar sizes. It also tells you how many of your profiles hold the coin, so you can see at a glance whether the rest of it is somewhere else.

## Reading the results

A bundle draws **one card** for the whole thing, and redraws it as each wallet's fill lands. Wallets that were left out are named separately, with the reason.

Individual taps each produce their own card, exactly like a normal trade.

<Info>
  Buying from five wallets spends five times the size you chose. The size applies to **each** ticked wallet; it is not a total divided between them.
</Info>

## Where the positions end up

Each wallet holds its own position with its own cost basis and its own PnL. The [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) shows the wallet you are in; switch wallet, or use the filters, to see the rest.

Orders are per-wallet too: a stop-loss set from one wallet protects that wallet's holding only.
