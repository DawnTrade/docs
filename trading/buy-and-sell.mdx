A market trade fills right away at the price available. It is the default way to trade in Dawn, and the fastest.

<Warning>
  **Tapping an amount sends the trade.** There is no confirm step, except on a coin Dawn [flags as risky](/trading/token-panel#risk-warnings), a buy that would [move the price more than 30%](/trading/execution#liquidity-protection) or lose a lot to network fees, or a trade that needs more gas than your [Gas Surplus](/settings/guardrails#gas-surplus) allows. Check the coin's address, the wallet and balance at the bottom of the panel, and your [slippage](/settings/overview#trading) (**15%** by default) before you tap.
</Warning>

## Buy a coin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the coin">
    Paste its contract address. Make sure the panel is on **Buying ⇄**.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/Paste-Contract-Address-in.png"
        alt="Paste Contract Address In"
        title="Paste Contract Address In"
        style={{ width:"58%" }}
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap an amount">
    The minimum is **\$1**. Ethereum and some cross-chain routes have a [higher one](/fees#minimum-trade).

    <Frame>
      ![Buy Submitted](/images/Buy-Submitted.png "Buy Submitted")
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  With [Auto Buy](/settings/overview#auto-buy) on, pasting a contract address buys a fixed amount straight away, without the panel step. It is off by default.
</Tip>

## Sell a coin

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your holding">
    Send `/portfolio` and tap the coin, or paste its address again.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/portfolio-1.png"
        alt=":portfolio"
        title=":portfolio"
        style={{ width:"79%" }}
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Switch to selling">
    If the panel is on **Buying ⇄**, tap it to switch to **Selling ⇄**. The sell buttons are **shares of what you hold**, not dollar amounts.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/sell-panel.png"
        alt="Sell Panel"
        title="Sell Panel"
        style={{ width:"61%" }}
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tap a share">
    **100%** sells the whole position. The USDC goes back onto your balance, ready to spend again.

    <Frame>
      <img
        src="/images/sell-card-1.png"
        alt="Sell Card"
        title="Sell Card"
        style={{ width:"66%" }}
      />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  There is no bulk exit across coins: sell each position from its own panel. For an exit that does not need you watching, set a [stop-loss or take-profit](/orders/tp-sl).
</Info>

## Why a trade gets refused

A failed trade is not a lost trade: your USDC is back on your balance, and the message says why. Most messages also say what to do. Two that need a change on your side:

| Reason | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| Price moved past your slippage | Raise [slippage](/trading/execution#slippage), or trade a smaller size. |
| Not enough balance | Deposit, or turn on [Auto-Adjust](/settings/guardrails#auto-adjust) to shrink the buy to what you can afford. |

More in [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting).
