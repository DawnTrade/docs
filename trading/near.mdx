NEAR coins trade from the same balance as every other chain. Your wallet gets a NEAR address the first time it trades there, and it shows under **NEAR** in `/wallets`.

## Open a NEAR coin

Paste the coin's NEAR address: a name ending in `.near`, or a 64-character id.

<Info>
  NEAR coins don't show in [search or Trending](/trading/trending), and pasting one never triggers [Auto Buy](/settings/overview#auto-buy).
</Info>

## Buying

Tap an amount as on any coin. Dawn pays from what your NEAR wallet already holds first, and moves the rest over from your USDC on Solana. When it has to move money across, the buy can take a few minutes, and its card says so while it waits.

A NEAR buy that fails leaves the money in your NEAR wallet, with no fee taken.

## Selling

A sell pays into your NEAR wallet as **USDC on NEAR**, or as **NEAR** for coins on Shards. It still counts in your **Balance**, and Dawn spends it on your next NEAR buy.

<Info>
  When your Solana USDC can't cover a buy on another chain or a USDC send, Dawn moves the difference over from your NEAR wallet first.
</Info>

## Move it back to Solana

Your NEAR wallet's NEAR and USDC each have their own row in your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a share">
    Tap **Move to USDC** on the row, with the share to move.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Tap **✅ Move to Solana**. It arrives as USDC in your Solana wallet within a few minutes, with no Dawn fee.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Under about **\$1** can't be moved.
