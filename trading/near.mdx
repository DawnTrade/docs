NEAR coins trade from your balance like any other coin. To open one, paste its NEAR address: a name ending in `.near`, or a 64-character id.

Your first buys can take longer while the money moves over from Solana. Once your NEAR wallet holds USDC, buys are fast, so [depositing on NEAR](#where-your-money-goes) gets you there from the start.

## Where your money goes

- A sell lands in your NEAR wallet as USDC on NEAR, or as NEAR for coins on Shards. It still counts in your **Balance**, and Dawn uses it for your next buy or send.
- NEAR or USDC you deposit by picking **NEAR** in `/deposit` lands there too.
- A failed or skipped NEAR buy leaves the money in your NEAR wallet, with no fee taken.
- To move it to Solana yourself, tap **Move to USDC** on its row in your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio).

## Send from your NEAR wallet

NEAR and USDC on NEAR can be sent to a NEAR account with `/send`, or **Transfer** on its row in your portfolio. Paste a name like `alice.near` or a 64-character id; `0x…` accounts aren't supported. Each send goes to one account, and there are no private sends from NEAR.

Sending USDC on NEAR costs you nothing; sending NEAR pays a small network fee in NEAR. A little NEAR always stays in the wallet to keep it working.
