NEAR coins trade from the same balance as every other chain. Your wallet gets a NEAR address the first time it trades there.

## Open a NEAR coin

Paste the coin's NEAR address: a name ending in `.near`, or a 64-character id.

<Info>
  NEAR coins don't show in [search or Trending](/trading/trending), and pasting one never triggers [Auto Buy](/settings/overview#auto-buy).
</Info>

## Buying

Dawn pays from what your NEAR wallet already holds first, and moves the rest over from your USDC on Solana.

## Selling

A sell pays into your NEAR wallet as **USDC on NEAR**, or as **NEAR** for coins on Shards. It still counts in your **Balance**, and Dawn spends it on your next NEAR buy.

To move it to Solana yourself, use **Move to USDC** on its row in your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio). It arrives as USDC in your Solana wallet.

<Info>
  When your Solana USDC can't cover a buy on another chain or a USDC send, Dawn moves the difference over from your NEAR wallet first.
</Info>
