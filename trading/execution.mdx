Slippage, MEV protection and gas are in `/settings` → [Trading](/settings/overview#trading). Each [profile](/wallets/profiles) has its own, so each can trade differently.

## Slippage

How far the price may move against you between your tap and the fill. Past that, the trade is refused and your USDC comes back. The default is **15%**.

Slippage is a limit, not a target: at 30% you agree to fill anywhere up to 30% worse, and on a calm pool you usually fill far inside it. Higher lets more trades through, some at a price you wouldn't have chosen; lower gets a better price, but fast movers bounce more often.

### Price impact

Price impact is your own trade moving the price, because you're buying a big share of the pool. It depends on your size against the pool's liquidity, and no setting removes it. Watch the **Liq** percentage on the [token panel](/trading/token-panel#the-market-block).

## Liquidity protection

Dawn refuses a buy that would move the price by more than **30%**. It stays on even at 100% slippage.

[Degen Mode](/settings/guardrails#degen-mode) turns it off and raises slippage to 100%, so a buy goes through however far it moves the price. Sells keep your normal slippage either way.

## MEV protection

**On** by default, Solana only. A public Solana trade can be seen before it lands, so a bot can buy in front of you and sell into the price your trade pushes up: a sandwich, which gets you a worse fill. With MEV protection on, your swaps go through a private lane, so nobody sees them in time.

## Gas preset

How hard your trades bid to land when blocks are busy. A higher bid doesn't improve your price; it improves your odds of landing while others compete for the same block.

| Preset | Use it for |
| --- | --- |
| 🐢 **Low** | A quiet chain when you're not racing anyone. Cheapest, but can be slower when the network is busy. |
| ⚡ **Medium** | Almost everything. The default. |
| 🚀 **High** | A launch, where landing in this block matters. |

<Info>
  Dawn pays this gas for you, up to [\$5 per trade](/fees#gas). The preset changes what Dawn bids, not what you pay, unless the cost goes over that cap: see [Gas Surplus](/settings/guardrails#gas-surplus).
</Info>
