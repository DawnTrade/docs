Send `/activity`, or `/history`, or tap **🕘 Activity** at the bottom of the [portfolio](/trading/portfolio). Where the portfolio is what you hold, Activity is the log: every filled buy and every filled sell, newest first.

## Reading a trade

```text
🟣 🟢 Buy $WIF
├ $50.00 → 1,240 WIF
└ 3h ago · Tx

🟣 🔴 Sell $WIF · 💳 Wallet 2
├ 620 WIF → $41.30 · 🟢 +$16.30 (+65.2%)
└ 12m ago · PnL Card · Tx
```

| Line | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy** | What you spent, and how many coins came back. |
| **Sell** | How many coins went out and what came back, then what that sell **realised** against the cost of those coins. |
| **PnL Card** | On a sell: renders the result as an image — see [PnL banners](/trading/pnl-cards). |
| **Tx** | The transaction on the chain's explorer. |

The wallet is named on a line only when the list spans more than one. Tap the ticker to open the coin's [panel](/trading/token-panel).

The log covers your most recent 200 trades.

## Sorting

**↕️ Sort** cycles three orders, and the button says which one is on:

| Sort | Puts first |
| --- | --- |
| **Newest** | The most recent trade — the default |
| **PnL** | The sell that made the most, in dollars |
| **PnL %** | The sell that made the most as a percentage |

Buys have no result, so under the PnL sorts they come after every sell.

## Narrowing the list

Two rows sit on the page itself and fold open with a tap:

| Fold | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 💳 **Wallets** | Show only the wallets you tick. Only there when the profile has more than one. |
| ⛓ **Chains** | Show only the chains you tick. |

**🔍 Filters** holds the rest:

| Filter | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Result** | All, **Wins** or **Losses**. Both look only at sells, since a buy has no result yet. |
| **Token** | Paste a contract address to narrow the list to that coin. Send `all` to clear it. |
| **↺ Reset filters** | Everything back to the default. |

The page says how many trades the filters left out, so nothing goes quietly missing. Filtering to losses is the more useful half of that pair, and the one most people skip.

## Closed positions

Activity is trade by trade. For the same history summed up **per coin** — what went in, what came back and the profit across every sell it took — switch the [portfolio](/trading/portfolio#realised-versus-unrealised) to **🚫 Closed**.

## What is not here

- **Open positions and their unrealised profit.** They are in the [portfolio](/trading/portfolio).
- **Copies that were skipped.** Copies a task placed are trades like any other and appear here; skipped ones are on the task's own [activity](/copytrade/manage#activity).
- **Coins you moved out.** Sending a coin to another address is not a sale, so nothing is realised.

<Note>
  A coin that came in from outside Dawn — transferred to your address rather than bought — has no purchase for Dawn to price against, so the profit on its sells may be incomplete. The figure is only as good as the history behind it.
</Note>
