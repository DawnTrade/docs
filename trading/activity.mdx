Send `/activity`, or tap **🕘 Activity** at the bottom of the [portfolio](/trading/portfolio). It lists every filled buy and sell, newest first, back to your last **200** trades.

Each sell shows what it **realised**: what came back, against what those coins cost you. **PnL Card** on a sell turns it into an image you can post: see [PnL banners](/trading/pnl-cards).

## Sorting and filters

You can narrow the list by wallet, chain, result (**Wins** or **Losses**) or a single coin, and sort it by PnL or PnL %. A buy has no result yet, so Wins and Losses leave buys out, and the PnL sorts put them after every sell.

<Tip>
  For the same history summed up per coin, switch the [portfolio](/trading/portfolio#realised-versus-unrealised) to **🚫 Closed**.
</Tip>

## What is not here

- **Open positions and their unrealised profit.** They're in the [portfolio](/trading/portfolio).
- **Copies that were skipped.** Copies a task placed are listed here like any other trade; skipped ones are in the task's own [activity](/copytrade/manage#activity).
- **Coins you sent out.** Sending a coin to another address isn't a sale, so nothing is realised.

<Note>
  A coin sent to your address from outside Dawn, rather than bought, has no purchase to price against, so the profit on its sells may be incomplete.
</Note>
