Send `/portfolio`, or tap **Portfolio** on the home screen. Tap a coin to trade it from its [token panel](/trading/token-panel), or **PnL Card** to turn it into an image you can post: see [PnL banners](/trading/pnl-cards).

## What the numbers mean

- **Invested** is your cost basis for what you still hold: what you spent on the tokens still in the wallet.
- **PnL** is always **net of fees**. The trading fee is already inside it, so the number you see is the number you'd have.
- What a position is worth is an **estimate** at the current price. What you actually get depends on liquidity when you sell; on a thin coin the gap can be large.

<Tip>
  Turn on **Fees in PnL** in [Trading settings](/settings/overview#trading) to also see what a position has paid in fees beside its profit. It changes what is shown, not what is counted.
</Tip>

## Realised versus unrealised

| | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Unrealised** | Profit on coins you still hold. It moves with the price and isn't yours until you sell. |
| **Realised** | Profit already banked by selling. It doesn't move again. |

The list shows both. Switch it to 🟢 **Open** for only what you still hold, or 🚫 **Closed** for positions you've sold out of, or down to a few cents, summed up per coin. For every buy and sell one by one, see [Activity](/trading/activity).

## Filters

**💳 Wallets**, **⛓ Chains** and **🔍 Filters** narrow the list, for example to hide dust, dead coins and coins with a pool under **\$10,000**.

<Note>
  Hiding a coin removes it from the list. It does not sell it, and it does not cancel its orders.
</Note>

## If a position is missing

1. **The wallet.** Positions belong to the wallet that bought them. Switch wallet, or tick it under **💳 Wallets**.
2. **The profile.** Switch with **Switch profile** on the home screen.
3. **The filters.** A minimum-worth or low-liquidity filter may be hiding it.
4. **Hidden tokens.** Show them under **🔍 Filters** and look again.

Still missing? Ask [support](/help/support).
