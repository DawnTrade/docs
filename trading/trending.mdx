Tap **🔥 Trending** on the home screen, or send `/trending`. To look a coin up by name or ticker, send `/search pepe`, or just type it, like `$PEPE`.

<Tip>
  Know the coin already? Paste its contract address instead. A ticker search is a guess; an address is exact.
</Tip>

## What the figures mean

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| **MC** | Market cap |
| **Liq** | How much money is in the coin's pool |
| **Vol** | Traded in the last 24 hours, and in the last **1h** |
| **✅ Dex paid** | The team bought the coin's DexScreener profile |

<Warning>
  **Dex paid** means someone paid for a listing, not that the coin is safe: a scam with a budget can buy one too. Read [what the panel cannot tell you](/trading/token-panel#what-the-panel-cannot-tell-you) before you buy anything you found here.
</Warning>

## Narrowing the list

- **Chain** shows that chain's own top coins.
- **Vol 24h** and **Trending** (the source's momentum ranking) change which coins you see. The other sorts only reorder them.
- 🎓 **Graduated** keeps only coins that have left their launchpad curve for a normal pool.
- ✅ **Dex paid** keeps only coins with a paid DexScreener profile.

Dawn remembers the chain and sort you last used.

<Info>
  Coins on chains Dawn can't trade never show up here. See the [chains Dawn trades](/chains).
</Info>
