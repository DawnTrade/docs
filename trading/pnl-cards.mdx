---

Tap **PnL banner** on the [token panel](/trading/token-panel) of a coin you hold, or **PnL Card** on a position in your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) or a sell in [Activity](/trading/activity). After a sell, the banner for it comes with the sell card on its own. Nothing is shared until you share it.

| From | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| A coin you still hold | The position as it stands, at the current price |
| A coin you sold out of | The finished result, across every sell it took |

## Percent or dollars

A banner shows the result as a percentage only, or with the dollar figures too. Percent is the one to post in public: it says how the trade went without saying how much you traded.

If **Fees in PnL** is on in [settings](/settings/overview#trading), the banner reflects it too.

<Tip>
  To share the coin with your referral link, so you earn from whoever buys it, use the panel's **Share** button instead. See [Share links](/social/share-links).
</Tip>
