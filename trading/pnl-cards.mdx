Tap **PnL banner** on the [token panel](/trading/token-panel) of a coin you hold, or **PnL Card** on a position in your [portfolio](/trading/portfolio) or a sell in [Activity](/trading/activity). After a sell, the banner for it comes with the sell card on its own. So does a [TP/SL](/orders/tp-sl) exit that sells your whole position. Nothing is shared until you share it.

| From | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| A coin you still hold | The position as it stands, at the current price |
| A coin you sold out of | The finished result, across every sell it took |

## Percent or dollars

A banner shows the result in dollars, or as a percentage. Percent is the one to post in public: in place of dollar figures it shows the market cap you bought at and the one you sold at, or the current one, so it says how the trade went without saying how much you traded.

If **Fees in PnL** is on in [settings](/settings/overview#trading), the banner reflects it too.

<Tip>
  To share the coin with your referral link, so you earn from whoever buys it, use **🔗 Share & Earn** under the coin's address on its panel instead. See [Share links](/social/share-links).
</Tip>

## Backgrounds

Pick what your banners are drawn on in `/settings` → **🖼 PnL Card Background**: one of Dawn's themes, or a photo, video or GIF of your own. Each [profile](/wallets/profiles) has its own.

With a video or GIF, your banners arrive as moving cards, except the one that comes with a sell card. Picking a theme later replaces your upload: to use it again, send it again.
