A PnL banner is a picture of one position's result, rendered as an image you can forward, post or save.

## Make one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the position">
    From the [token panel](/trading/token-panel) of a coin you hold, or from the card Dawn sends after you sell out of one.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Tap PnL banner">
    Dawn asks how you want the result shown.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Pick a format">
    | Format | Shows |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Percent** | The gain or loss as a percentage only |
    | **Dollars** | The dollar figures as well |

    Percent is the one to use in public. It says how the trade went without saying how much you were trading.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Check it before you post it">
    The image arrives in your chat. Nothing is shared anywhere until you share it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Open and closed positions

| From | Shows |
| --- | --- |
| A coin you still hold | The position as it stands, at the current price |
| A coin you sold out of | The finished result, across every sell it took |

If **Fees in PnL** is on in [settings](/settings/overview#trading), the banner reflects it too.

<Note>
  **One card at a time.** Ask for another within a few seconds and Dawn asks you to wait a moment.
</Note>

## Sharing it with your link

A banner is an image on its own. To share the coin *and* earn from whoever buys it, use the panel's **Share** button instead — that produces a card with your referral link attached. See [Share links](/social/share-links).
