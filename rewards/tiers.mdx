You're on a rung of two separate ladders. Your own trading never moves your referral tier, and your network never moves your cashback tier.

## Cashback ladder

Set by **your own** 30-day trading volume. The effective fee is what the [1% trading fee](/fees#the-trading-fee) costs you after [cashback](/rewards/cashback).

| Tier | 30-day volume | Cashback | Effective fee |
| --- | --: | --: | --: |
| **Silver** | \$0 – \$8.5k | 25% | 0.75% |
| **Copper** | \$8.5k – \$85k | 30% | 0.70% |
| **Ruby** | \$85k – \$450k | 35% | 0.65% |
| **Gold** | \$450k – \$2M | 40% | 0.60% |
| **Platinum** | \$2M – \$10M | 45% | 0.55% |
| **Diamond** | \$10M – \$50M | 50% | 0.50% |
| **Ultra** | \$50M+ | 55% | 0.45% |

## Referral ladder

Set by the 30-day volume of the people **you invited directly**. Only Level 1 climbs. Each share is of the fee left after the trader's own cashback — see [what the share is taken from](/rewards/referrals#what-the-share-is-taken-from).

| Tier | 30-day referred volume | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | --: | --: | --: | --: |
| **Silver** | \$0 – \$450k | 25% | 3% | 2% |
| **Ruby** | \$450k – \$4.5M | 30% | 3% | 2% |
| **Gold** | \$4.5M – \$22M | 35% | 3% | 2% |
| **Platinum** | \$22M – \$90M | 40% | 3% | 2% |
| **Diamond** | \$90M+ | 50% | 3% | 2% |

<Info>
  The same name means different things on each ladder: **Ruby** starts at \$85k of your own volume, but at \$450k of referred volume.
</Info>

## How the window works

Both ladders count only the **last 30 days**, so your rung follows what you've done recently, not a lifetime total. Dropping a rung changes what you earn from then on; nothing already credited is taken back.

Your rung and your progress to the next are on the **Rewards** screen. Earnings from both are claimed together — see [claiming](/rewards/overview#claiming).
