Open **Rewards → Referrals** for your link, your network and your earnings.

## The three levels

You earn a share of the trading fees paid by:

| Level | Who they are | Your share |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **Level 1** | People you invited yourself | **25% – 50%**, by tier |
| **Level 2** | People *they* invited | **3%** |
| **Level 3** | One level deeper again | **2%** |

## Tiers

Your tier is set by the **30-day trading volume of the people you invited directly**. Your own trading doesn't count, and only your Level 1 share grows with it. Every rung and the volume it takes: [All tiers](/rewards/tiers).

## What the share is taken from

Your share is of the fee **left after the trader's own cashback**, not of the whole fee.

## Sharing your link

Your link is on the Rewards screen, and **📤 Share Referral Link** posts it into a chat. Anyone who starts Dawn through it is your referral permanently.

On the Referrals screen you can switch between two styles of the same link: ✈️ **Telegram Link** (`t.me/DawnTradeBot?start=…`) opens the bot in one tap inside Telegram, and 🌐 **dawn.trade Link** (`dawn.trade/@yourcode`) reads better in bios and posts.

<Tip>
  A coin's [share card](/social/share-links) carries your link too, and whoever buys through it becomes your referral. You can also [put Dawn in a group](/social/groups), or run [several codes](/rewards/codes) to see which channel works.
</Tip>

## Getting paid

You earn as your network trades: someone who joins and never trades earns you nothing, even though they count on your Rewards screen. Referral earnings join your cashback in one claimable balance. See [Claiming](/rewards/overview#claiming).
