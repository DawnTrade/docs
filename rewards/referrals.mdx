Invite someone to Dawn and you earn a share of the trading fees they pay — and a smaller share from the people **they** invite, and from the level below that.

Open **Rewards → Referrals** for your link, your network and your earnings.

## The three levels

| Level | Who they are | Your share |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| **Level 1** | People you invited yourself | **25% – 50%**, by tier |
| **Level 2** | People *they* invited | **3%** |
| **Level 3** | One level deeper again | **2%** |

Level 1 is the one that grows with your tier. Levels 2 and 3 are flat, and they are where a network starts paying for itself — they need nothing from you at all.

## Tiers

Your referral tier is set by the **30-day trading volume of the people you invited directly**. Your own trading does not affect it.

| Tier | 30-day referred volume | Level 1 | Level 2 | Level 3 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Silver** | \$0 – \$450k | 25% | 3% | 2% |
| **Ruby** | \$450k – \$4.5M | 30% | 3% | 2% |
| **Gold** | \$4.5M – \$22M | 35% | 3% | 2% |
| **Platinum** | \$22M – \$90M | 40% | 3% | 2% |
| **Diamond** | \$90M+ | 50% | 3% | 2% |

Like [cashback](/rewards/cashback), the window rolls continuously and the tier moves with it. Both ladders side by side: [All tiers](/rewards/tiers).

## What the share is taken from

Your share is of the fee **left after the trader's own cashback**, not of the whole fee.

That ordering matters, and it is the fair way round: the person doing the trading is paid first, and referral earnings come out of what remains.

## Sharing your link

Your referral link is on the Rewards screen. Tap it to copy.

Anyone who starts Dawn through it is attributed to you permanently.

There are two better-converting ways to share than a bare link:

## Getting paid

Referral earnings join your cashback in one claimable balance. Claim from the Rewards screen — minimum **\$5**, once an hour, paid as USDC onto your balance.

<Note>
  Referral earnings are credited as the people in your network trade. Someone who signs up and never trades earns you nothing; the count on your Rewards screen shows who joined, not who is active.
</Note>
