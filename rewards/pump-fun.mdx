Coin cashback comes from the coin, not from Dawn. It applies to your buys and sells in that coin, on top of Dawn's own [cashback](/rewards/cashback) and your [referral earnings](/rewards/referrals).

## Spotting one

A cashback coin's [token panel](/trading/token-panel#coins-still-on-a-launchpad-curve) shows a **💸 Cashback coin** line with the rate. Each coin sets its own.

## Collecting it

You do nothing. Dawn claims it for you, pays the network fee, and messages you when a payment lands.

| The coin is quoted in | You're paid |
| --- | --- |
| SOL | **SOL**, in your wallet. [Move it to your balance](/funds/move-to-balance) to trade with it. |
| USDC | **USDC**, straight onto your balance |

<Warning>
  Cashback doesn't make a coin worth buying. The rate is a launch setting anyone can pick, and it's tiny next to what the price can do. Judge the coin on [the rest of its panel](/trading/token-panel) first.
</Warning>
