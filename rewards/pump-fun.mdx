Some pump.fun coins are launched so that a share of **every trade** goes back to whoever traded it. This is separate from Dawn's own [cashback](/rewards/cashback) — it comes from the coin, not from Dawn.

## Spotting one

A coin that does this says so on its [token panel](/trading/token-panel#coins-still-on-a-launchpad-curve), on the line above its stats:

```text
💸 Cashback coin: 0.5% of every trade back to you
```

The percentage is set by the coin, and it applies to your trades in it — buys and sells both.

## Collecting it

You do nothing. Dawn claims what you are owed for you, and pays the network fee to do it.

When a claim lands you get a card in the chat with the amount and a link to the transaction.

| The coin is quoted in | You are paid in | It lands |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SOL | **SOL** | In your wallet — [move it to your balance](/funds/move-to-balance) to trade with it |
| USDC | **USDC** | Straight onto your balance |

<Note>
  SOL landing in the wallet rather than on the balance is the usual case. It is real money either way; it just needs one tap before you can spend it in Dawn.
</Note>

## How it stacks up

These rewards are independent and they add together:

| Source | Paid by | On what |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Coin cashback | The coin | Your trades in that coin |
| [Dawn cashback](/rewards/cashback) | Dawn | The fee on all your trades |
| [Referrals](/rewards/referrals) | Dawn | Fees paid by your network |

<Warning>
  A coin paying cashback is not a coin worth buying. The rate is a launch parameter anyone can set, and it is a rounding error next to what the price can do. Judge the coin on [everything else on its panel](/trading/token-panel) first.
</Warning>
