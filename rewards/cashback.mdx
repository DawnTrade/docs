Every buy and sell pays a [1% trading fee](/fees#the-trading-fee). Your tier is set by your **own trading volume, buys and sells, over the last 30 days**. The people you invite raise your [referral tier](/rewards/referrals) instead.

## Tiers

Cashback starts at **25%** (an effective fee of **0.75%**) and climbs with your volume to **55%** (**0.45%**) at the top tier. Every rung and the volume it takes: [All tiers](/rewards/tiers).

Your tier and your progress to the next one are under **Rewards → Cashback**.

## Getting paid

Cashback joins your referral earnings in one claimable balance. See [Claiming](/rewards/overview#claiming).
