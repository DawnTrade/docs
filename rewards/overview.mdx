Open it with **🎁 Rewards** on the home screen, or send `/rewards`. There are two kinds of reward, earned separately and paid into one **claimable** balance.

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="Cashback" icon="rotate-left" href="/rewards/cashback">
    A share of **your own** trading fee back to you: **25%** to **55%**, by your 30-day volume.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Referrals" icon="user-plus" href="/rewards/referrals">
    A share of the fees paid by people you invite, and by the people **they** invite, three levels deep.
  </Card>
</Columns>

## Claiming

Tap **Claim** on the Rewards screen. The payout lands as **USDC on your balance**, usually within a minute.

- Minimum claim: **\$5**
- Once an hour at most
- Rewards don't expire. Anything under the minimum keeps adding up.

## Tiers

Each kind has its own tier ladder, based on the last 30 days:

|  | Tier set by | Paid on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Cashback** | Your own 30-day volume | Your own trades |
| **Referrals** | Your direct referrals' 30-day volume | Their trades, and their referrals' |

Every rung and what it takes: [All tiers](/rewards/tiers).

<Info>
  Some pump.fun coins also pay a share of every trade back to the people trading them, and Dawn claims it for you. See [Pump.fun cashback](/rewards/pump-fun).
</Info>
