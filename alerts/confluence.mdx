Open it with **🔔 Alerts** → **🎯 Confluence** on the home screen or in `/alerts`. You can have several rules, each watching its own traders, and they cover all your profiles.

## Add a rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Whose buys count">
    **🏅 Top traders** watches everyone on fomo's [leaderboards](/copytrade/find-trader#top-traders), and follows the boards as they change. **✏️ Own list** watches only the fomo traders you pick.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Your traders">
    Own list only: the traders the rule watches.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Time frame">
    How close together the buys must land. **10 min** unless you change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Traders needed">
    How many different traders must buy the coin within that time frame. **3** unless you change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Cooldown">
    How long a coin stays quiet after it alerts. **6h** unless you change it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What counts

- Only buys count; sells never do.
- Each trader counts once per coin, however many times they buy it.
- Buys on Solana and every EVM chain count.
- A coin's cooldown covers all your rules: when several match the same coin, you get one alert.

## Your rules

Open a rule from **🎯 Confluence** to turn it on or off, change its settings, add or remove traders, or delete it. Whether it watches top traders or your own list can't be changed: make a new rule.

## Limits

- Up to **10 rules**.
- Up to **25** traders of your own per rule, on an own list or added to the top traders.
