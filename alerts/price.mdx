Open your price alerts with **🔔 Alerts** → **📈 Price** on the home screen or in `/alerts`.

## Set an alert

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Automations">
    On the coin's panel, on the buying side, tap **⚙️ Automations**, then **🔔 Alert**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the level">
    The market cap or price to alert at.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Alerts work on any coin, whether you hold it or not. When the coin reaches your level, from above or below, Dawn messages you once and the alert ends.

## Manage your alerts

**📈 Price** lists every alert you have and how long each has left. **Edit** moves one to a new level and gives it a fresh 7 days.

## Limits

- **5** alerts per coin, **25** per wallet.
- Each lasts **7 days**, then ends without a message.
