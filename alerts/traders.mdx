Trader Alerts keep you in the loop on the traders you rate. Each buy, sell or thesis they post lands in your chat as it happens, with a button to trade it yourself. Nothing is bought for you.

Tap **🔔 Tracker** on the home screen, or send `/tracker`.

<Note>
  Two features sound similar. To have a trader's buys placed for you, use [Copytrade](/copytrade/overview). To hear when a coin reaches a market cap, set a [price alert](/alerts/price) — that is **🔔 Price Alerts**, beside **🔔 Tracker**.
</Note>

## Add a trader

<Steps>
  <Step title="Who to track">
    Tap **➕ Add a Trader** and send one of these:

    | Send | What you hear about |
    | --- | --- |
    | A **Solana address** | That wallet |
    | An **EVM address** (`0x…`) | That wallet, on the chains you pick — or **All Chains** |
    | A **fomo handle** — `@name`, or a fomo.family profile link | Their wallets, on every chain Dawn trades |
    | A **pump.fun profile** — a link or a username | Their pump.fun wallet |
    | A **screenshot** of their fomo profile | Dawn reads the handle off the picture |

    For an EVM address, tap the chains to hear about, then **Continue →**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name them">
    Keep the suggested name, or type your own — up to **24 characters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick what to hear">
    | Button | You hear about |
    | --- | --- |
    | **📈 Trades** | Their buys and sells |
    | **📝 Theses** | What they post on fomo and pump.fun |
    | **📈 + 📝 Both** | All of it |
  </Step>
</Steps>

Their page opens and alerts start straight away. Theses only come from traders who are on fomo or pump.fun — pick **📝 Theses** for someone on neither, and you get their trades instead.

Already looking at a trader? The cards from [Find a trader](/copytrade/find-trader), and the card you get for any wallet you paste into the chat, have a **🔔 Alerts** button that fills them in for you.

## What you'll get

### Trades

| Alert | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| 🔔 **New Buy by …** | What they spent and got, whether it is their **first buy** of the coin or they are **buying more**, and the coin's market cap, liquidity and price |
| 🔔 **New Sell by …** | The share they sold, what they got for it, and what they have left |

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **⚡ Buy \$TICKER** | Opens the coin's buy panel |
| **🤖 Copy This Trader** | Starts a [copytrade task](/copytrade/overview#follow-a-trader) with the trader filled in |

### Theses

A thesis is a trader's write-up of why they are in a coin.

| Alert | Posted on |
| --- | --- |
| 📝 **New Thesis by …** | fomo |
| 📝 **New Callout by …** | pump.fun |

Each one shows what they wrote, how their own position in the coin is doing, and the market cap now next to where it was when they posted. **✅ verified** marks a verified account, and **⚠️ deployed this coin** means they created the coin they are writing about.

**⚡ Trade** opens the coin's panel right in the alert.

To read what everyone has written about a coin, tap **📝 Thesis** on its panel — see [Top theses](/trading/token-panel#top-theses).

## Your traders

**🔔 Tracker** lists everyone you follow, with 🟢 for live or 🔴 for paused and what you hear about. **Manage** opens a trader's page, and **Pause** or **Resume** switches them off or on.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **➕ Add a Trader** | Follow someone new |
| **Resume All** / **Pause All** | Every trader at once |

### A trader's page

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **Pause** / **Resume** | Stop every alert for this trader, or start them again |
| **🔗 Share Task** | Pass this trader to someone else |
| **📝 Theses: On** / **Off** | Thesis and callout alerts |
| **📈 Trades ▾** | Unfolds **Buys: On / Off** and **Sells: On / Off** |
| **🔍 Trade Filters** | Which of their buys you hear about — see [Trade filters](#trade-filters) |
| **🗑 Delete** | Stop following them, after a confirm |

One alert always stays on. To go quiet on a trader completely, **Pause** them.

## Trade filters

Filters narrow which **buys** you hear about. Sells and theses always come through.

| Filter | No alert when | Type it as |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 🌱 **First Buys Only** | It is not their first buy of the coin — adds and re-entries are left out | Tap to switch on |
| 🎯 **Buy Min** | They spent less than this | `250` for \$250 |
| 💎 **MC Min** | The coin is smaller than this | `50k`, `$1.5m` |
| 💎 **MC Max** | The coin is bigger than this | `5m` |
| 💧 **Liq Min** | The pool holds less than this | `20k` |
| 📊 **Vol 24h** | Less than this traded in the last day | `100k` |
| 👥 **Holders** | Fewer holders than this | `200` |
| 🐋 **Top 10** | The top ten wallets hold more than this share | `30` for 30% |
| ⏱ **Age Min** | The coin is younger than this | `30m`, `12h`, `30d`, `2w` |

Type a value, or `off` to clear it. **♻️ Reset Filters** clears them all. A coin with no data for a filter yet sends no alert.

## Limits

|  |  |
| --- | --- |
| Traders | **50** per wallet, separate from your copytrade tasks. A trader followed on several chains counts once for each chain. |
| One per trader | The same wallet cannot be tracked twice |
| fomo theses | On for up to **10** traders at a time |
| pump.fun callouts | On for up to **50** traders at a time |
| Name | Up to 24 characters |
