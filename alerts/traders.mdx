Open it with **🔔 Alerts** → **👥 Traders** on the home screen or in `/alerts`, or send `/tracker`.

<Info>
  To have a trader's buys placed for you, use [Copytrade](/copytrade/overview). To hear when a coin reaches a market cap, set a [price alert](/alerts/price).
</Info>

## Add a trader

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the trader">
    Tap **➕ Add a Trader**, send who you want to follow, and name them. You can follow the same kinds of trader as in [Copytrade](/copytrade/overview#who-you-can-copy). For an EVM wallet, you also pick its chains.

    A name on both fomo and pump.fun asks which trader you mean. **Both** follows each of them.
  </Step>
  <Step title="What to hear">
    **📈 Trades**, **📝 Theses**, or both. Alerts start right away.

    <Info>
      Theses only come from traders on fomo or pump.fun. Pick **📝 Theses** for anyone else and you get their trades instead.
    </Info>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What you'll get

### Trades

- 🔔 **New Buy**: when they buy. Narrow which buys with [trade filters](#trade-filters).
- 🔔 **New Sell**: when they sell.

### Theses

A thesis is a trader's write-up of why they're in a coin.

- 📝 **New Thesis**: when they post one on fomo.
- 📝 **New Callout**: when they post one on pump.fun.

<Tip>
  To read what everyone has written about a coin, see [Top theses](/trading/token-panel#top-theses).
</Tip>

## Your traders

`/tracker` lists everyone you follow. **Manage** on one lets you pause them, choose which alerts you get, set [trade filters](#trade-filters), share them or delete them.

<Info>
  One alert always stays on. To go quiet on a trader completely, **Pause** them.
</Info>

## Trade filters

Filters narrow which **buys** you hear about; sells and theses always come through. They are the same as [copytrade filters](/copytrade/manage#filters), without 🥇 **First Buy Wins**. A buy that fails one, or a coin with no data for it yet, sends no alert.

## Limits

- Up to **50 traders** per wallet, separate from your copytrade tasks. A trader followed on several chains counts once per chain.
- The same wallet can't be tracked twice.
- fomo theses can be on for up to **10** traders at a time, pump.fun callouts for up to **50**.
