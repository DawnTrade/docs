Dawn charges one fee, on trades, and covers your gas up to a cap. Every other cost comes from the network or the market, not from Dawn.

## <u>The trading fee</u>

**1%** of every buy and sell, with a **\$0.35** minimum. Each [TWAP](/orders/twap) slice is its own trade and pays it separately.

[Cashback](/rewards/cashback) pays part of it back: **25%** at the entry tier, rising to **55%** with your 30-day volume. That makes the effective fee **0.75%**, down to **0.45%** at the top tier.

## <u>Gas</u>

Dawn pays up to **\$5 of gas per trade**, which covers nearly every trade on every chain. You never need to hold SOL, BNB, ETH or HYPE to trade.

When a trade needs more than that, your [Gas Surplus](/settings/guardrails#gas-surplus) setting decides:

| Gas Surplus | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| **Off** (default) | The trade is declined before it is sent. Nothing is spent. |
| **Set to a limit** | The extra comes out of your USDC, up to your limit. Past it, the trade is declined. The limit can be up to **\$50** per trade. |

<Warning>
  Take-profit and stop-loss sells follow the same limit. A stop that needs more gas than you allow will not fire.
</Warning>

## <u>Minimum trade</u>

**\$1**. Routes that move value between chains have a higher minimum, and the bot tells you when a trade is below it.

## <u>Trading on another chain</u>

Your balance is USDC on Solana, so buying a coin on any other chain means moving value across. That routing has its own cost. It is not a Dawn fee and Dawn does not absorb it, but it is already inside the quoted price.

It weighs far more on a \$5 trade than on a \$200 one. A Solana coin bought from your balance has no such cost, and neither does a NEAR coin paid for from what your [NEAR wallet](/trading/near) already holds. Moving money from NEAR back to Solana has no Dawn fee.

## <u>Withdrawals</u>

Dawn charges no fee on withdrawals or sends. You pay only the network's costs:

| Sending | What you pay |
| --- | --- |
| **From Solana** | The gas, plus creating the recipient's token account if they have never held the token. Paid in USDC from your balance, even when you send SOL or another token. |
| **From an EVM chain** | The gas, in that chain's own coin, from your wallet on that chain. |
| **To another chain** | The gas, in USDC. The confirmation shows the amount that will arrive. |

See [Withdraw and send](/funds/withdraw).

## <u>Other costs</u>

None of these is a Dawn fee. They move the price you actually get.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Pool fees and price impact" icon="chart-line">
    Every swap pays its liquidity pool, typically 0.01%–1%, and every trade of size moves the price against itself. Both come out of the amount you receive.

    On thin liquidity this can cost far more than the trading fee. Check **Liq** on the [token panel](/trading/token-panel#the-market-block) before a big trade.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Token taxes" icon="circle-exclamation">
    Some tokens charge their own tax on every buy or sell, sometimes 10% or more, sometimes only on selling. It is taken before you receive the coins, not shown as a separate charge.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## <u>Failed trades</u>

A trade that cannot fill inside your slippage is refunded: your USDC comes back and no trading fee is charged.

A buy on another chain can fail after it has gone out. Your USDC and the trading fee come back to your balance on their own, less any gas the attempt used. A failed NEAR buy leaves its money in your NEAR wallet, with no fee taken.

Any other gas spent on a failed trade is covered by Dawn.
