---
title: "Glossary"
description: "Plain definitions for the words Dawn uses."
icon: "book"
---

| Term | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **2FA** | A second check — an authenticator code, a passkey or an SMS code — before a send goes out or a key is exported. Never asked for a trade. |
| **Approve** | Confirming a send or withdrawal yourself in the wallet app. A send you do not approve within five minutes expires. |
| **Auto Buy** | Pasting a contract address buys a fixed amount immediately. Off by default. |
| **Auto-Adjust** | Shrinks a buy you cannot afford to what your balance covers, instead of refusing it. |
| **Balance** | Your spendable USDC. Every trade comes out of it and every sell goes back into it. |
| **Bonding curve** | The launchpad market a coin trades on before it graduates to a normal pool. |
| **Bundle** | Several Solana buys landed in one block at one price, all or nothing. |
| **Cashback** | A share of your own trading fee, returned to you. 25%–55% by tier. |
| **Cashback coin** | A coin that pays a share of every trade back to whoever traded it. |
| **Copytrade** | Following a wallet so that its buys, and optionally its sells, are placed from your own wallet too. |
| **Cost basis** | What you actually paid for the tokens you still hold. |
| **DCA** | Buying a fixed amount on a repeating schedule. Driven by time, not price. |
| **Degen Mode** | 100% slippage and no liquidity protection on buys. |
| **Dex paid** | The team bought this coin's DexScreener profile. A sign that someone spent money on the listing, not a sign of safety. |
| **Dust** | A position too small to be worth anything, usually hidden by a filter. |
| **fomo** | fomo.family, a social trading app. Dawn can copy or follow a fomo trader from their handle alone. |
| **Gas** | What the network charges to process a transaction. Dawn pays it, up to \$5 a trade. |
| **Gas Surplus** | How much gas above that \$5 you allow a trade to charge you. |
| **Graduated** | A coin that has left its launchpad curve and now trades on a normal pool. |
| **Ladder** | Several orders spread across a market-cap range. |
| **Leader** | The wallet a copytrade task follows. |
| **Limit order** | A buy or sell that waits for a market cap you set. |
| **Liquidity** | How much money is in a coin's pool. Thin liquidity means your own trade moves the price. |
| **Market cap** | What the whole supply of a coin is worth. Every order target in Dawn is set against this. |
| **MEV** | Value extracted by reordering transactions — a bot buying in front of you and selling into your trade. |
| **MEV protection** | Sending Solana trades through a private lane so nobody can do that. On by default. |
| **Mirror sells** | A copytrade setting: when the leader sells a share of a coin, you sell the same share of yours. |
| **Missed copy** | A copy Dawn skipped because the price was already further above the leader's entry than your slippage allows. |
| **PnL** | Profit and loss. Always shown net of fees. |
| **Portfolio weighted** | A copytrade size: the same share of your portfolio as the leader put in of theirs. |
| **Price alert** | A message when a coin reaches a market cap or price you set. Nothing is traded. |
| **Price impact** | How far your own trade moves the price. Set by your size against the liquidity. |
| **Profile** | A set of trading settings shared by its wallets. Up to 5. |
| **Realised** | Profit you have banked by selling. It will not move again. |
| **Rung** | One order in a ladder. |
| **Sandwich** | A bot buying before your trade and selling after it, taking the difference. |
| **Slippage** | How far the price may move against you before a trade is refused. Default 15%. |
| **Stop loss** | An automatic sell after a fall. |
| **Take profit** | An automatic sell after a rise. |
| **Task** | One followed trader in copytrade, with its own name, size and settings. |
| **Tax** | A charge written into a coin's own contract, taken on every buy or sell. |
| **Thesis** | A trader's written case for a coin, posted on fomo. On pump.fun it is a callout. |
| **Trader alert** | A message when a trader you follow buys, sells or posts a thesis. Nothing is bought for you. |
| **TWAP** | One order split into small trades spread over a duration you choose. |
| **Unrealised** | Profit on coins you still hold. It moves with the price. |
| **Wallet** | One EVM address and one Solana address, with their own balance, positions and orders. Up to 5 per profile. |
| **Wallet manager** | The mini app inside Telegram where keys are imported and exported, sends are approved, 2FA is set up and a card deposit starts. |
| **Worth** | What a position would be valued at right now. An estimate, not a guaranteed sale price. |
