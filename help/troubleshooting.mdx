Most problems come down to being in a different **wallet** or **profile** than you think: balances, positions and orders belong to a wallet, and wallets belong to a profile. For anything that failed, read the reason on its card first.

## Money

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="I deposited and my balance has not changed" icon="arrow-down-to-bracket">
    Check which wallet you deposited into, and which profile it's in: **Ready** on the home screen only counts the wallets in the profile you're in.

    **SOL**, and any coin someone sent you, lands in the wallet rather than on your balance. [Move it across](/funds/move-to-balance) from the wallet screen.

    Check the route's minimum on the deposit panel, and open the panel right before you send: a deposit sent long after still lands, but its card may not follow.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My NEAR sale is not in my Solana USDC" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    NEAR sales, failed NEAR buys and NEAR deposits stay in your NEAR wallet and still count in **Ready**. See [Trading on NEAR](/trading/near).
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I sent to the wrong chain or the wrong address" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Sent to a Dawn address on a chain Dawn supports? Open `/deposit` and check: it may still be credited or refunded. Anywhere else, it's gone. Nobody can reverse an on-chain transfer.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A withdrawal was rejected as too small" icon="paper-plane">
    Withdrawals to another chain start at \$1, and the gas comes on top of what you typed unless you send `max`.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A private withdrawal has not arrived" icon="user-secret">
    Its card in the chat says where it stands. Nothing is sent until you tap **🔐 Approve in app**, and the legs then go out over the timing you chose. **⏩ Send now** pays the waiting legs straight away.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Trades

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Buy failed**: price moved past your slippage | Raise your [slippage](/trading/execution#slippage), or trade smaller. |
| Not enough balance | Check the wallet in use. [Auto-Adjust](/settings/guardrails#auto-adjust) shrinks a buy to what you can afford instead of refusing it. |

## Orders

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My order did not fire and the level was passed" icon="clock">
    If the price **gapped** through your level by more than your slippage allows, Dawn doesn't fill into the gap. The order stays armed until the price comes back into range.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My stop-loss did not fire" icon="shield-halved">
    Check it in `/orders`. Gas rarely holds one back: a stop pays up to [\$50 of extra gas](/settings/guardrails#gas-surplus) on its own.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find an order I placed" icon="magnifying-glass">
    `/orders` shows the wallet and profile you're in, and orders keep running in every profile. Switch profile from the home screen and look again. Price alerts have their own list in `/alerts` → **📈 Price**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Copytrade

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My task is not copying anything" icon="robot">
    Open the task's **🕘 Activity**: every skipped copy is listed with its reason. The usual ones: the task is **paused**, a **filter** skips the buys, a percent-sized copy comes to **under \$1**, or the task's wallet has **no balance**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A mirrored sell did nothing" icon="rotate">
    It sells the coin the task bought, from the task's wallet. If you already sold it, a TP/SL sold it, or the task never bought it, there is nothing to sell. With **🤝 Hold if Down** on, a sell is also held back while you're at a loss, fees included.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My copy filled far from the leader's price" icon="chart-line">
    Your copy lands after the leader's buy, up to your slippage above the price they paid. Lower the task's slippage to stay closer to their entry; copies past it are skipped. A smaller size, a liquidity floor in the task's [filters](/copytrade/manage#filters) and a stop loss also help.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Alerts

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A trader's alerts have gone quiet" icon="bell">
    Open the trader from **🔔 Alerts** → **👥 Traders** and check they aren't **paused**. Picked **📝 Theses** when you added them? Their trades are off: switch on **Buys** or **Sells** under **📈 Trades**. **🔍 Trade Filters** can hold back buys, never sells or theses.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="A price alert never came" icon="chart-line">
    Price alerts last **7 days**, then end without a message. One still listed in `/alerts` → **📈 Price** hasn't been reached yet.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Positions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A coin I hold is not in my portfolio" icon="chart-pie">
    Check the wallet and profile, then the **filters**, **hidden tokens** and [scam tokens](/trading/portfolio#filters). A minimum-worth or low-liquidity filter hides rows without removing them. A coin you've sold down to a few cents moves to 🚫 **Closed**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My PnL looks wrong" icon="calculator">
    Worth is an estimate at the current price: on a thin coin, a sale can fetch well below it. Fees on your buys only count with [Fees in PnL](/settings/overview#trading) on. A coin that arrived from outside Dawn has no purchase to price against, so its cost basis may be incomplete.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot sell a coin" icon="lock">
    Some contracts block transfers, or block selling for everyone except the deployer. No setting in Dawn changes that. Dawn [warns before you buy](/trading/token-panel#risk-warnings) the coins it can catch, not every one.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Panels and buttons

| Issue | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A button is missing | Check which side the panel is on, and whether the feature needs more than one wallet. |
| Dawn did not recognise the address | [Name the chain](/chains#naming-the-chain-yourself) yourself: `bnb 0x…`, `base 0x…`, `arc 0x…`. |
| The mini app shows the wrong account | Close it and reopen it from the Telegram account you use with Dawn. |
