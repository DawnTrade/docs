Open it with `/copytrade`, or **🤖 Copytrade** on the home screen. Each trader you copy is a **task** with its own size and settings.

## Who you can copy

- A **Solana wallet**
- An **EVM wallet** (`0x…`), on the [chains](/chains) you pick
- A **fomo trader**: their `@handle`, profile link, or a screenshot of their profile. They are copied on every chain they trade.
- A **pump.fun trader**: their profile link or username

<Tip>
  Not sure who to copy? [Find a trader](/copytrade/find-trader) shows how a trader or wallet has been doing first. To get pinged on a trader's moves with nothing bought for you, use [Trader Alerts](/alerts/traders).
</Tip>

## Follow a trader

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick the trader">
    Tap **➕ Follow a Trader**, send who you want to copy, and name the task. For an EVM wallet, you also pick its chains.

    A name on both fomo and pump.fun asks which trader you mean. **Both** makes a task for each, with the same settings.

    Made a task before? **✅ Same as Last Time** reuses its settings and skips the steps below.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy amount">
    | Sizing | Each copy spends |
    | --- | --- |
    | **A fixed amount** | The same dollar amount every time |
    | **📊 % of the Leader's Buy** | A share of what the leader spent |
    | **⚖️ Portfolio Weighted** | The same share of your portfolio as the leader put in of theirs, scaled by a ratio you pick |

    <Accordion title="Portfolio Weighted example">
      The leader puts 5% of their \$40,000 into a coin. You have \$800, so you put in 5% of yours: **\$40**. At a **2x** ratio, it's **\$80**.
    </Accordion>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Max per buy">
    For **📊 %** and **⚖️ Portfolio Weighted** sizing. The most one copy can spend; a bigger copy is cut down to it. You can add a minimum later on the [task page](/copytrade/manage#min-and-max-per-buy).
  </Step>

  <Step title="What to copy">
    | Choice | When the leader sells |
    | --- | --- |
    | **Buys Only** | Nothing. You sell when you want. |
    | **Buys + Sells** | You sell the same share of yours, in profit or at a loss. |
    | **🤝 Buys + Sells, Hold When I'm Down** | You sell with them while you're in profit, fees included. If you're down, you keep the position. |
    | **↩️ Hold When Down, Exit at Break-even** | The same, and once the price is back to what you paid, fees included, you sell the share they sold. |
    | **🔄 Reverse: Buy When They Sell** | You buy the coin they sold, and their buys are ignored. See [Reverse copytrade](#reverse-copytrade). |

    <Info>
      **Hold When I'm Down** never sells at a loss for you, so pair it with a stop loss.
    </Info>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Slippage">
    How far above the leader's price your copy may buy. Past that, the copy is [missed](#missed-copies). **15%** suits most tasks; **Off** copies at any price. The task's stop losses and trailing sells use it too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stop loss and take profit">
    Optional. Added to every buy the task makes. Trailing sells and [Dev Sell](/orders/tp-sl#dev-sell) can be added later under the task's **📈 Auto Sell**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Minimum market cap">
    Optional. Skips coins smaller than this. More [filters](/copytrade/manage#filters) are on the task page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Create">
    Check the summary and tap **✅ Create task**. Copying starts right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  A copy is a trade you didn't look at first. Size tasks with that in mind.
</Warning>

## Notifications

While a task runs, Dawn messages you when a copy:

- 🟢 **Fills**: a buy or sell went through
- 🔴 **Fails**: nothing was bought or sold, and your USDC is back on your balance
- ⏩ **Is skipped**: for example, because of a filter, not enough balance, or a coin [flagged as risky](/trading/token-panel#risk-warnings)
- ⏩ **Is missed**: the price had already run past your slippage

Every copy is also listed in the task's [activity](/copytrade/manage#activity).

## Missed copies

Still want in after a miss? Tap **⏳ Limit at Leader's Entry** on the message. It sets a [limit buy](/orders/limit) at the price the leader paid, for 24 hours.

To do this on every miss, turn on **📌 Limit Order On Miss** in the task's [settings](/copytrade/manage#settings).

## Reverse copytrade

With **🔄 Reverse: Buy When They Sell**, the task buys the coins the leader sells, sized from what their sale brought in. Their buys are ignored, so you exit with **📈 Auto Sell** or by hand.

- **🥇 First Buy Wins** starts on, so a leader selling in several pieces gives you one buy.
- Slippage counts from the leader's sell price, and a missed copy offers **⏳ Limit at Leader's Exit** instead.
- A task is reverse from the start or not at all: to switch, make a new task.

## Your tasks

`/copytrade` lists all your tasks, across every wallet and profile. Tap **Manage** on one to pause it, change its size or settings, or see what it has done — see [Managing a task](/copytrade/manage).

Each task buys from the wallet you made it on.

## Limits

- Up to **50 tasks** per wallet. A trader copied on several chains counts once per chain.
- Copies under **\$1** are skipped.
- One task per trader on each wallet.
