Copytrade watches a wallet you name and trades when it does. Each buy that wallet makes becomes a buy of your own, sized the way you chose and paid from your own balance. Ask for it and their sells are mirrored too.

Send `/copytrade`, or tap **🤖 Copytrade** on the home screen. A followed trader is a **task**: it has a name, a size and its own settings, and it keeps running whether or not Telegram is open.

## Who you can copy

| Paste | What Dawn follows |
| --- | --- |
| A **Solana address** | That wallet, on Solana. |
| An **EVM address** (`0x…`) | That address on the EVM chains you tick — BNB Chain, Robinhood Chain, Base, Arc and Ink. One task covers every chain you picked. |
| A **fomo handle** — `@name`, or a fomo.family profile link | The wallets behind that trader, copied on every chain they use. |

Paste a wallet address into the chat on its own and Dawn notices it is a wallet rather than a coin: the reply offers **🤖 Copytrade This Wallet**, which drops you straight into setup with the address filled in.

### fomo traders

[fomo](https://fomo.family) shows a trader's swaps but hides the wallet behind them. When you paste a handle, Dawn matches those swaps against what happened on chain and works out the wallet. It takes a few seconds, and the card says so while it looks.

| Card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| ✅ **Found @name** | Their Solana wallet is listed and, if they have ever bridged, their EVM wallet too. The task copies them on every chain they trade. |
| 🤷 *Their wallet can't be worked out yet* | They are on fomo but have not traded enough to pin down. Try again after they trade, or paste the address if you have it. |
| ❌ *Couldn't find them* | No such handle. Check the spelling. |

A resolved trader is checked again every couple of weeks in case they moved wallets. Individual chains can be turned on or off later from the task — see [Chains](/copytrade/manage#chains).

## Follow a trader

<Steps>
  <Step title="Who to copy">
    Tap **➕ Follow a Trader** and paste the address or handle. For an EVM address, tick the chains to copy it on — or **All Chains** — and **Continue**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Name the task">
    Dawn suggests the handle or the short address. Keep it, or type your own — up to **24 characters**.

    <Note>
      Already have a task? Dawn offers **✅ Same as Last Time** first: the new task takes every setting from the one you made most recently, and only asks for a name. **🛠 Set Up Fresh** walks through the steps below.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Buy amount">
    How much each copied buy spends. Either a **fixed dollar amount** — your [buy presets](/settings/presets) are the buttons, or type any figure — or **📊 % of the Leader's Buy**.

    In percent mode you pick the share — **5% · 10% · 25% · 50% · 100%**, or any whole percent from 1 to 500 — and then a **max per buy**, so a whale-sized buy never becomes one of yours: **\$10 · \$25 · \$50 · \$100**, your own figure, or **No cap**.
  </Step>

  <Step title="What to copy">
    **Buys Only** — you decide when to sell. **Buys + Sells** — when they sell, you sell the same share of yours. This is the **Mirror Sells** setting, and you can flip it later.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Slippage">
    How far the price may move before a copy is refused. **10% · 15% · 20% · 30%**, or type your own between 0.1% and 50%. **15%** suits most — a leader moving a memecoin pool needs more room than a quiet trade of your own.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Stop loss and take profit">
    Two steps, each a quick pick, a typed level, or **⏭ Skip · Off**. A level set here is attached to **every buy the task copies**, in the same forms as any [TP/SL](/orders/tp-sl#setting-a-level) — `-30%`, `2x`, `+150%`.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Minimum market cap">
    Skip buys into coins smaller than this. **50K · 100K · 250K · 1M**, a figure like `100k`, or **⏭ Skip · No Minimum**. More [filters](/copytrade/manage#filters) live on the task page.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and create">
    Everything you chose, on one card. **✅ Create task** and the task is live: its page opens, and the next thing the trader buys is yours too.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every step has a **Back** button, so a slip does not mean starting over.

## How a copy is placed

- Dawn watches the followed wallet and reacts the moment a swap lands. A swap Dawn only sees **30 seconds** or more after it happened is left alone — the price it was made at is gone.
- A **buy** spends your size: the fixed amount, or your share of what the leader spent, capped. A copy that works out under **\$1** is skipped rather than sent.
- A **sell**, with Mirror Sells on, sells the **same share of your position** that the leader sold of theirs — they sold half, you sell half; they sold out, you sell out. If you hold none of the coin there is nothing to do, and the copy is skipped. Sells are never filtered.
- Each copy is an ordinary market trade from the task's wallet, with the task's own [slippage, MEV protection, gas and live fees](/copytrade/manage#settings), and pays the normal [trading fee](/fees).
- A copied buy that **reverted on chain** — the pool moved between the quote and the landing — goes out **once more** on a fresh quote at the task's slippage, inside the same 30-second window. It fills in the range you set, or not at all.
- A stop loss or take profit set on the task is attached to every buy it fills. Copies never take the profile's default exits.

<Warning>
  A copy is a trade you did not look at. The leader's entry is not yours: by the time your buy lands the price has moved, sometimes a lot, and on a thin coin your own copy moves it further. Size tasks for that, and set a stop loss on any task you will not be watching.
</Warning>

## What lands in your chat

| Card | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| 🟢 **Copytrade buy filled** | Your copy went through. The card names the task, what you spent and received, and what the leader did — *Leader bought: 0.5 SOL · 1.2M \$PEPE*. A **📉 Sell** button sits on top for a quick exit. |
| 🟢 **Copytrade sell filled** | A mirrored sell went through, with the share sold and what came back. |
| 🔴 **Buy failed** / **Sell failed** | It was sent and the chain refused it. The card names the reason, and your USDC is back on your balance. |
| ⏩ **Copytrade Skipped** | The copy was never sent, and the card says why: a filter the coin failed, a size under \$1, not enough balance. |

A skipped-by-filter card comes **once an hour per task and coin**, so a busy leader under a strict filter does not fill your chat; every skip is still in the task's [activity](/copytrade/manage#activity). Not enough balance gets a **💰 Deposit** button.

## The task list

`/copytrade` lists every task across all your wallets and profiles: a 🟢 or 🔴 for live or paused, the size, the chain or chains, the wallet it spends when that is not the one you are in, and two links — **Manage** opens the [task page](/copytrade/manage), **Share** hands the trader to someone else.

| Button | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| **➕ Follow a Trader** | Starts the setup above |
| **▶️ Resume All** / **⏸ Pause All** | Every task at once |
| **🕘 Activity** | Every copy across every task — see [Activity](/copytrade/manage#activity) |
| **🗑 Delete** | Pick a task to remove |

## Limits

| | |
| --- | --- |
| Tasks | **50** per wallet. A fomo trader takes one slot for each chain they are copied on. |
| Size | At least **\$1** per copy. Percent mode 1%–500%, cap optional. |
| Name | 1–24 characters, and not one another task already uses. |
| One task per trader | The same wallet on the same chain cannot be followed twice from one of yours. |

A task spends the wallet it was created on, and the positions it opens land there. Tasks keep running whichever wallet or profile you have selected. Everything about a running task can be changed afterwards — see [Managing a task](/copytrade/manage).
