Whoever you look up, you can start a [copytrade task](/copytrade/overview#follow-a-trader) or [trader alerts](/alerts/traders) for them from there.

## Look up a fomo trader

Tap **🔎 Find a Trader** on the Copytrade page and send their fomo `@handle`, their fomo.family profile link, or a screenshot of their profile. You get their wallets and their PnL on fomo. Pasting their profile link straight into the chat does the same.

## Check a wallet

Paste any wallet address into the chat. A fomo trader's wallet shows them as above. Any other wallet that has traded in the last 30 days shows its trading record; its **Realized PnL** is the profit it banked by selling.

## Top traders

**🏆 Top Traders** on the Copytrade page opens fomo's leaderboard inside Telegram, ranked by profit on fomo over 7 days, 30 days or all time. Tap a trader for their stats and a backtest; **Copy trader** takes you back to Dawn with setup ready.

<Tip>
  A big share of PnL from **1 coin** they still hold usually means one lucky bag rather than steady trading.
</Tip>

### Backtest a trader

The backtest replays a trader's recent buys as if you had been copying them.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Starting balance">
    **Start with \$**: what you begin with. \$1,000 unless you change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Size per copy">
    **Each copy \$**: what every copied buy spends. \$50 unless you change it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Window">
    **7D** or **30D**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sells">
    Turn **Copy sells** on or off, then tap **Run backtest**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Past results don't promise future ones. A real copy buys at the price when it lands, not the price the trader got.
</Note>
